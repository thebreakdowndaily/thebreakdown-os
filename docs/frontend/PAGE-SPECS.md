# The Breakdown — Page Specifications

---

## 1. Homepage (`/`)
- **Environment**: Dark Discovery (`#0E0D0B`).
- **Key Sections**:
  1. `Navigation`: Fixed top bar with wordmark, IA links (`Library`, `Investigations`, `Explainers`, `Topics`, `Data`, `The Brief`), search shortcut `/`, profile menu.
  2. `HeroSection`: Asymmetrical 2-column layout (Playfair Display 3.5rem headline, Source Serif 4 dek, inline evidence indicators, and Knowledge Panel with period bar).
  3. `TrustBar`: Dark charcoal bar with live platform audit signals (Chapters Reviewed, Claims Registered, Primary Sources, Last Verified).
  4. `The Brief` (`ShortVersionGrid`): Numbered briefing layout (`01`, `02`, `03`) with category color accents and time-to-read.
  5. `TopicHubs`: 6-column horizontal mineral strip showcasing living topic dossiers.
  6. `Knowledge Library` (`LatestChapters`): Archival 2fr/1fr asymmetric layout featuring Chapter 01 "India's Inheritance" with evidence grade badge and upcoming chapter status pills.
  7. `MissionBar`: Deep research three-pillar foundation explaining evidentiary rigour.
  8. `NewsletterBand`: Intimate warm reading section for The Breakdown Brief weekly intelligence signup.
  9. `Footer`: Institutional 4-column governance and exploration layout.

---

## 2. Investigations Index (`/investigations`)
- **Environment**: Dark Discovery (`#0E0D0B`) with Atmosphere Blue accents (`#1E2D40` / `#5B8AB0`).
- **Header**: Editorial statement emphasizing evidence-first reporting rather than opinion.
- **Content**: Grid of investigative pieces or editorial In-Research roadmap showing upcoming volume topics.
- **Cards**: Highlight chapter counts, publication dates, and primary investigation focus.

---

## 3. About Institutional Platform (`/about`)
- **Environment**: Dark Discovery.
- **Layout**: Asymmetric 2-column format (60% editorial declaration, 40% governance links & reading paths).
- **Sections**:
  - Foundational mission declaration.
  - Pullquote: *"Read it like a newspaper. Understand it like an explainer. Verify it like a researcher."*
  - Four numbered institutional principles.
  - Direct pathways to Editorial Constitution (v1.1), Sourcing Methodology, and Trust Dashboard.

---

## 4. 404 Error Experience (`/not-found`)
- **Environment**: Deep Research (`#0A0908`).
- **Visual Anchor**: Large archival `404` in Playfair Display with ochre accent rule.
- **Tone**: Calm, editorial, and helpful.
- **Recovery**: Integrated search bar and quick-navigation chips to Library, Investigations, and Topics.
