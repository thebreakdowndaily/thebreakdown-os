# ERD-0001: HOMEPAGE PROMOTED TO "THE BEGINNING"

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Governance Level:** Level 3 Experience System  

## Context
Traditional digital publishing treats the initial entry URL as a "homepage"—a directory or catalog of disconnected articles. This design mental model encourages information overload, chronological news feeds, and surface scanning.

## Decision
The entry surface of The Breakdown OS is officially renamed and reconceptualized as **"The Beginning" (The Opening Scene of Act One)**.

## Consequences
- The surface is designed as a streamlined narrative trailer (4 core scenes) rather than an article index.
- All internal documentation, navigation labels, and experience specifications refer to "The Beginning".
- Chronological news feeds are permanently eliminated from the surface.
