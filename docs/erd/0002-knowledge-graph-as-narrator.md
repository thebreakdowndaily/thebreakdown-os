# ERD-0002: KNOWLEDGE GRAPH RECONCEPTUALIZED AS NARRATOR

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Governance Level:** Level 3 Experience System  

## Context
Data visualisations in traditional media are typically static diagrams or isolated widgets. Readers interact with them passively without experiencing narrative connection.

## Decision
The Knowledge Graph is reconceptualized from a passive visual component into an active **Character and Narrator**.

## Consequences
- The Knowledge Graph illuminates connected nodes dynamically as the reader progresses through narrative scenes.
- The Knowledge Graph possesses Narrative Memory, retaining the reader's personal map of systemic understanding across sessions.
- Graph animations must educate and guide, serving as the narrative voice of the platform.
