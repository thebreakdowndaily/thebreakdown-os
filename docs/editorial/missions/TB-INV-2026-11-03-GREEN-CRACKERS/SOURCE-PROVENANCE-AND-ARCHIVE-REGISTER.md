## 2. Archival and Evidence Integrity Safeguards

1. **No-RTI Compliance:** Every source in this register was accessed through publicly accessible web URLs, official government portals, or published journalism. No RTI requests were filed, drafted, or queued.
2. **Attribution Boundary:** Clear distinction is preserved between:
   - Primary legal mandates (Supreme Court, NEERI)
   - Official institutional statements (CPCB, Delhi Police public index)
   - Secondary investigative journalism (*Hindustan Times*, *The Indian Express*)
3. **No Fabricated Provenance:** No field inspection, undercover purchase, or physical product scan was conducted by The Breakdown desk.
4. **Permanent Digital Vault:** For publication under Level 1 Editorial Constitution Article III, primary sources will be bound to canonical archive IDs in the Evidence Vault upon formal human ingestion.
