## Verification Rules Applied
- **Rule A (No Orphan Claims):** Every material assertion must be traceable to primary or verified secondary evidence.
- **Rule B (No Citation Laundering):** Secondary repetitions are not independent corroborations.
- **Rule C (No False Precision):** Non-reconciled numbers from differing districts and windows must not be summed.
- **Rule D (No Unsupported Causality):** Temporal correlation (before vs after) is not scientific causation.
- **Rule E (No False Completeness):** Partial records must be explicitly marked as partial.
- **Rule F (No Source Inflation):** Third-party reporting is never presented as The Breakdown's own fieldwork.
- **Rule G (Preserve Disagreement):** Competing accounts and unresolved institutional roles are preserved.
- **Rule H (No Fabricated Observation):** No visits, purchases, or QR scans are claimed unless documented.
