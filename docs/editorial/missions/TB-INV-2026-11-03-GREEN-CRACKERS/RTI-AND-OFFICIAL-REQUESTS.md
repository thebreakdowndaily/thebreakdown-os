## Routing: use the correct portal

The Central RTI Online portal explicitly says not to file requests for State Government authorities, including Government of NCT Delhi; such applications may be returned without refund. It lists the following central public authorities relevant to this investigation:

- CSIR-National Environmental Engineering Research Institute (NEERI)
- Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO)
- Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- Delhi Police

Use https://rtionline.gov.in/ and select the exact authority from its current list. The official list currently names CSIR-NEERI, PESO, CPCB and Delhi Police.

For Government of NCT Delhi departments / district administration, use the Delhi RTI system and identify the relevant PIO:
- Delhi Government RTI information and PIO directory: https://delhigovt.nic.in/rti/
- Delhi RTI-MIS: https://rtionline.delhi.gov.in/RTIMIS/login/

Do not use the Central RTI portal for GNCTD departments, DPCC if routed as a Delhi authority, or Delhi district administration. For Haryana and Uttar Pradesh NCR districts, use the respective state RTI routes and district/department PIOs; do not assume Delhi jurisdiction covers the entire NCR.

Before filing, re-check the portal's current authority list, PIO details, fee and format. The templates below request existing records, not explanations, opinions or creation of new data. If an item is not held by the chosen authority, request transfer under section 6(3) where legally applicable, or identify the authority that holds it.

## Filing protocol and legal hygiene

1. File separate requests with the correct public authority. Avoid one oversized application to a department that does not hold all records.
2. Ask for existing records, copies, registers, datasets, minutes and electronic files. RTI is not a mechanism to compel an authority to answer research questions, create a new analysis or give an opinion.
3. Keep the date range narrow and explicit. For later data, submit a supplementary request once the reporting period closes.
4. Save the application PDF, payment receipt, registration number, submission timestamp and every response.
5. If information is denied, note the exact provision invoked and consider a first appeal within the statutory period. The usual RTI response period is 30 days, subject to statutory exceptions and applicable rules; verify the current rules and calculate the deadline from the filing record.
6. Do not publish personal data, security-sensitive operational details or exempt information merely because it appears in a response. Assess the public-interest basis, statutory exemptions and redactions.
7. A response saying “no record available” is evidence only of the authority's stated record position. It is not proof that the activity did not occur.
8. For NCR districts outside Delhi, file separately with the relevant Haryana or Uttar Pradesh authority. The Court's NCR directions are not limited to GNCTD.
