- Attribute third-party market reporting; do not claim independent observation.

- Do not infer that no record exists merely because a portal search did not surface it.
- No causal attribution of AQI or hospital changes to fireworks without a defensible method.
- No RTI applications, fees, submissions or requests requiring applicant identity.
