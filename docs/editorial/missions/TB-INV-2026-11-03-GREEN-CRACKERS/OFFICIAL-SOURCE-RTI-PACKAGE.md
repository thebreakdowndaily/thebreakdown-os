## 1. Priority finding: QR-code responsibility needs clarification

The Supreme Court's 29 October 2026 order requires every box to carry a product- and batch-specific, non-transferable QR code. On scanning, it should display manufacturer, NEERI registration, PESO/DM licence, formulation and test certificate. The order also directs authorities to randomly verify QR-code authenticity.

CSIR-NEERI's official press release dated 16 October 2025 says:
- Product-specific QR codes are to be developed by green-firework manufacturers.
- Regulatory agencies such as PESO and CPCB verify those codes.
- CSIR-NEERI issues registration letters and does not itself provide QR/barcodes on products.

These statements are not necessarily irreconcilable: the 2026 order may impose a more detailed product/batch-level requirement, while the 2025 release describes the earlier QR arrangement. Do not claim a legal conflict or system failure without an official explanation. Obtain the current protocol, responsible agency, database/verification mechanism and transition record.

Sources:
1. Supreme Court, *Arjun Gopal v. Union of India*, order dated 29 October 2026, 2026 INSC 1181: https://indiankanoon.org/doc/113136598/
2. CSIR-NEERI press release, 16 October 2025: https://www.neeri.res.in/file_homes/35509814_Press%20Release_Green%20firecrackers.pdf
3. CSIR-NEERI Green Firecrackers National Centre of Excellence: https://www.neeri.res.in/neeris/details/green-firecrackers-national-centre-of-excellence

## 3. Filing principles

- Each application below is a separate, narrowly scoped request to one public authority.
- Ask for existing records, documents, registers, datasets, file notings, orders, reports, minutes and correspondence. Do not ask the PIO to answer broad questions, give opinions, conduct research or create a new analysis.
- State a date range and request electronic copies (PDF/CSV/XLSX) where held.
- If information is held by another public authority, request transfer under Section 6(3) of the RTI Act, 2005 and notification of the transfer.
- If any part is denied, request the specific exemption and severability decision under Section 10, with the First Appellate Authority details.
- If records are voluminous, request inspection under Section 2(j) and then identify selected pages/files for copies.
- Do not include private personal data of traders, patients or individual officers beyond what is necessary and lawfully disclosable. Seek aggregate health information and official action records.
- RTI applications are not substitutes for urgent safety complaints. For a current suspected violation, use the official complaint channel displayed by local authorities and preserve a timestamped record.
- RTI applications generally receive a response within 30 days under Section 7(1), subject to statutory exceptions. Do not assume an RTI filed on 3 November will return before the 4–6 November sale window. Parallel written media questions are necessary.

## 12. Evidence handling protocol

For every record received:
- Preserve original email/PDF/download and original filename.
- Record source authority, PIO/official, receipt date, application/dispatch number and date range covered.
- Hash the original file (SHA-256) in the newsroom evidence log.
- Record whether the document is signed, digitally signed, unsigned, draft, provisional or final.
- Distinguish a department's assertion from an underlying register, raw dataset or independently verified record.
- Maintain a claim-to-source ledger with page/paragraph/row references.
- Redact personal data before publication; retain unredacted originals in restricted evidence storage.
- Request clarification if date, unit, denominator, station coverage or definition is unclear.
- Do not interpret non-response as proof of non-compliance.
