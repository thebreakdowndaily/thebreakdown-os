## 2. Environmental Monitoring Ingestion & Attribution Protocol

### A. Data Retrieval Targets
1. **Hourly Station-Level Concentrations:** Export raw hourly PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NOx, CO, and Ozone for all 40+ CAAQMS stations in Delhi and NCR across the three windows (2–6 Nov, 7–8 Nov, 9–30 Nov).
2. **Quality Control & Uptime Auditing:** Filter for station dropout rates, missing hourly values, and CPCB quality-control validation flags. Apply the Delhi Government 2-hour missing station exclusion rule explicitly.
3. **Chemical Speciation & Tracer Analysis:** Retrieve laboratory findings on pyrotechnic tracer elements (barium, strontium, potassium, magnesium, copper) from collected filter papers.

### B. Causal Attribution Guardrails
- **The Confounder Matrix:** Never compare raw pre- and post-Diwali AQI numbers without controlling for:
  - Planetary boundary layer height and ventilation index;
  - Wind speed, surface temperature inversions, and wind direction vectors;
  - Regional agricultural stubble burning (fire counts and satellite aerosol optical depth from NASA FIRMS / VIIRS);
  - Baseline urban emissions (traffic volume and industrial activity).
- **Categorization Standard:**
  - *Data Not Yet Published:* Mark all post-8 November measurements as pending until officially released.
  - *Data Published But Unverified:* Provisional CAAQMS portal exports must be labelled "provisional real-time data."
  - *Data Independently Verified:* Validated multi-station data reconciled against weather models.
  - *Unsupportable Claims:* Assertions that fireworks alone caused a specific percentage spike without chemical speciation models.

## 3. Public Health Data Verification Protocol

### A. Health Dataset Audit
1. **Facility Network:** Document which hospitals submitted records (government tertiary hospitals, municipal facilities, private chains).
2. **Clinical Stratification:** Separate data into four distinct clinical cohorts:
   - Acute pyrotechnic blast and ocular trauma;
   - Cutaneous thermal burns;
   - Acute exacerbation of pre-existing chronic respiratory disease (COPD, asthma);
   - New-onset pediatric/adult respiratory distress.
3. **Baseline Comparison:** Reconcile reported admission figures against:
   - Average admissions during the identical calendar period in non-bursting ban years (2023–2025);
   - Pre-Diwali baseline week (2–6 Nov 2026).

## 4. December Judicial Review & Compliance Audit

### A. 14 December Affidavit Examination
Upon filing of sworn affidavits by the Centre, GNCTD, Police, and CPCB:
1. Reconcile reported total seizures against the fragmented district media quotes reported in early November.
2. Check whether authorities identify the designated retail sales locations that were missing from public portal indexes.
3. Check whether PESO chemical laboratory analysis reports on retail samples confirmed or disproved the presence of banned barium salts.
4. Verify the number of random retail QR scans conducted and their verified authentic match rate.

### B. 16 December Court Reporting
1. Attend or review official cause lists, bench proceedings, and the resulting operative court order.
2. Publish an evidence-linked follow-up investigation assessing whether authorities satisfied the Supreme Court's compliance test or whether judicial strictures were renewed.
