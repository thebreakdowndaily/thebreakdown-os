## 1. Editorial Briefing & Intent

This investigation examines the regulatory, legal, and environmental reality behind the Supreme Court of India's decision to permit a temporary, tightly restricted window for certified green crackers across Delhi-NCR for Diwali 2026 (*Arjun Gopal v. Union of India*, 2026 INSC 1181).

Rather than framing the issue as an ideological debate over festival traditions or treating court orders as self-executing reality, the story investigates the **enforcement and evidence architecture**:
1. **The Regulatory Calendar:** The Court established segmented operational windows (manufacture 2–6 Nov, retail sale 4–6 Nov, bursting 7–8 Nov for 3 hours daily, complete ban restored 9 Nov).
2. **The Field Breakdown:** Reporting by *Hindustan Times* and remarks in open court documented that unapproved fireworks were sold openly before the licensed sales window, QR code verification failed in multiple market hubs, and Delhi Police acknowledged continuous residential patrols are not feasible.
3. **The Evidence Gap:** In 2025, authorities failed to present satisfactory implementation and environmental data following a similar relaxation. The true accountability test will arrive on 14 December 2026 when CPCB monitoring datasets and hospital respiratory admission records are due before the Supreme Court.
