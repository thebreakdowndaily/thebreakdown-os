## 4. Fee Calculation & Test-Incentive Interpretation

1. **Daily Fee Structure (20 September 2021 Apex Council Table):**
   - **Tier 1 (Up to 20 matches):** ₹40,000/day (Playing XI) / ₹20,000/day (Reserves).
   - **Tier 2 (21 to 40 matches):** ₹50,000/day (Playing XI) / ₹25,000/day (Reserves).
   - **Tier 3 (More than 40 matches):** ₹60,000/day (Playing XI) / ₹30,000/day (Reserves).
2. **Ranji League Season Arithmetic:**
   - 7 matches × 4 days = 28 playing days.
   - Playing XI calculation: ₹11,20,000 (Tier 1), ₹14,00,000 (Tier 2), ₹16,80,000 (Tier 3).
   - **Crucial Editorial Safeguard:** This is explicitly framed in Chapter 2 as *conditional arithmetic* for a cricketer selected in the XI for every league day, not an annual guaranteed salary. It excludes taxes, state deductions, washouts, and benchings.
3. **Test Cricket Incentive Scheme vs. Domestic Daily Fees:**
   - Standard Test match fee is ₹15 lakh per match.
   - March 2024 scheme adds:
     - Playing >50% (>4 of a 9-Test season): +₹30 lakh/match.
     - Playing >75% (>6 of a 9-Test season): +₹45 lakh/match.
   - **The Four-Test Ambiguity:** A player appearing in 1 to 4 Tests receives zero incentive bonus, earning the baseline ₹15 lakh/match. The story keeps this structural threshold explicit.
4. **Three Separate Economic Instruments:**
   - The analysis strictly refuses to conflate: (a) Domestic daily fees, (b) Central annual retainers (₹1 cr to ₹7 cr), and (c) Test match incentives.

## 6. Known Evidentiary Limitations & Caveats

1. **Tax and Net Earnings:** BCCI match fee vouchers are subject to TDS and state association overheads; net take-home earnings are not published.
2. **Institutional Employment:** A subset of domestic cricketers holds public sector / banking employment (e.g. Railways, CAG, Indian Oil) providing basic stipends independent of BCCI match fees.
3. **Amay Khurasiya's Position:** The ₹1 lakh/day recommendation is an informed coaching perspective rather than a formal economic feasibility study; the story clearly flags this distinction.
