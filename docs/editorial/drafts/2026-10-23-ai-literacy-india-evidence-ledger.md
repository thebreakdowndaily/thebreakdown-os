## Explicit non-claims

This draft does **not** claim that Indian schoolchildren generally cannot verify AI outputs; that AI has improved or harmed measured learning outcomes; that any particular model is inaccurate; that CBSE curriculum is implemented uniformly; or that CSF/CBSE declined to comment. None of those claims is established by the sources consulted here.
