## 1. Executive Summary & Design Principle

Phase V-1C establishes the **Comprehension Validation Instrument** for The Breakdown Knowledge Platform.

### 🚫 Non-Proxy Mandate
Conventional web metrics (**time-on-page**, **scroll depth**, **click-through rate**, **page completion**) are explicitly rejected as proxies for learning. A reader who scrolls to the bottom of an article in 45 seconds has executed gestures, but has not necessarily understood anything.

The Comprehension Instrument measures **cognitive learning outcomes directly** through pre/post inquiry probes and 9 evaluation dimensions.

---

## 2. The 9 Core Comprehension Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Core Evaluation Question | Target Cognitive Capability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Central Question Recall** | *"What core question did this investigation explore?"* | Identifies the governing inquiry without confusing it with secondary details. |
| **2. Evidence Recognition** | *"Which specific document or dataset supports the lead assertion?"* | Links claims directly to primary sources rather than accepting un-sourced assertions. |
| **3. Three-Layer Discrimination** | *"Which part of the assertion is a Documented Fact vs. Historical Interpretation vs. Editorial Synthesis?"* | Distinguishes established empirical floor from historiographical debate and synthesis. |
| **4. Uncertainty Recognition** | *"What remains unresolved or subject to ongoing scholarly debate?"* | Identifies epistemic limits and boundaries of existing evidence. |
| **5. Counterargument Recognition** | *"What is the strongest competing explanation or criticism?"* | Evaluates opposing interpretations fairly without single-narrative bias. |
| **6. Causal Understanding** | *"Why did this historical or policy outcome occur?"* | Explains multi-variable causality rather than memorizing isolated dates/facts. |
| **7. Evidence Navigation** | *"How can you locate the primary treaty text or audit report for this claim?"* | Demonstrates independent descent from narrative claim to Tier 1 source provenance. |
| **8. Transfer** | *"How does this principle apply to a related historical/policy case?"* | Applies conceptual understanding to novel, closely related inquiries. |
| **9. Metacognitive Shift** | *"What prior assumption did this evidence complicate or change?"* | Reflects on personal learning trajectory and updated prior beliefs. |

## 3. The Multi-Dimensional Understanding Delta Model

Understanding is modeled as a 6-vector delta between pre-exposure baseline and post-reading evaluation:

$$\vec{U}_{\text{Delta}} = \vec{U}_{\text{Post}} - \vec{U}_{\text{Pre}}$$

### The 6 Independent Comprehension Dimensions

```text
1. Factual Comprehension [0 – 100] ──────► Recall of verified empirical assertions
2. Causal Comprehension [0 – 100] ───────► Understanding of multi-variable cause-and-effect
3. Uncertainty Awareness [0 – 100] ──────► Recognition of missing data & open debates
4. Evidence Literacy [0 – 100] ──────────► Ability to trace Tier 1 primary sources
5. Counterargument Awareness [0 – 100] ─► Fair evaluation of competing schools of thought
6. Spatial & Structural Orientation [0–100] ► Navigation awareness across knowledge graph nodes
```

> **Governance Invariant:** The 6 dimensions MUST remain separately observable. They must NOT be collapsed into a single reductionist "engagement score" or "learning index".

## 4. Evaluation Probe Structure per Journey

### Sample Probe Set — Journey A (Non-Alignment & Early Cold War)

1. **Pre-Inquiry Baseline Probe (Prior Belief):**
   - *"What was the main reason India chose Non-Alignment in 1947–1961?"*
   - Options: (A) Ideological isolation, (B) Assertion of sovereign strategic autonomy, (C) Passive neutrality, (D) Anti-Western alliance.
2. **Post-Inquiry Three-Layer Probe:**
   - *"Categorize the following assertions from the text:"*
     - Assertion 1: *"The 1961 Belgrade Summit was attended by 25 sovereign states."* → **Documented Fact**
     - Assertion 2: *"Non-Alignment sacrificed military modernization prior to 1962."* → **Historical Interpretation**
     - Assertion 3: *"India's strategic autonomy laid the foundation for modern multipolar diplomacy."* → **Editorial Synthesis**
3. **Metacognitive Reflection Probe:**
   - *"What is one assumption you held before reading that the evidence in Chapter 1 complicated?"*
