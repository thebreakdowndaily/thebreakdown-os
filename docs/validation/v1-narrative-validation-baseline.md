## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

The Breakdown has completed its **Platform-Wide Narrative Migration** (Phases W-0 through W-8), establishing a canonical 4-scene narrative homepage ("The Beginning"), global spatial breadcrumbs (`SpatialNarrativeBreadcrumb`), and analytical knowledge projections across all 47 mapped routes.

**Phase V-1 (Narrative Experience Validation & Reader Comprehension Pilot)** is initiated NOT to redesign or add infrastructure, but to answer one central question with empirical rigor:

> *"Does The Breakdown's Narrative Operating System measurably improve reader understanding while preserving evidence visibility, uncertainty, accessibility, agency, and independent research capability?"*

In accordance with **Phase V-1A directives**, no reader-facing production code was modified during this audit, no new analytics infrastructure was created, and no shadow profiling was introduced.

## 2. Answers to the 7 Core Baseline Audit Questions

### Question 1: What can already be measured by the repository?

1. **Technical Performance & Reliability:**
   - Core Web Vitals (LCP target <1200ms, CLS = 0.0, TTFB).
   - Infrastructure SLAs (99.9% uptime, error rates <0.02%, CDN cache hit ratio >97.8%).
   - Security headers, rate limiting, script sanitization, and cryptographic SHA-256 provenance hashes.
2. **Basic Telemetry & Interaction Counter:**
   - Pageviews (`story:view`, `topic:view`), top queries via `PluginAnalyticsService` and `MemoryAnalyticsPlugin` (`services/analytics/service.ts`).
   - Workspace state (`tb_last_story`) stored strictly in reader local storage.
3. **Automated Accessibility Telemetry:**
   - Static WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA structure profile reports generated by `WCAGComplianceProfileBuilder` (`lib/accessibility/compliance-projection.ts`) and audited in `tests/accessibility.test.ts`.
4. **Editorial Quality & Epistemic Completeness:**
   - Gold Standard 7-Phase Audit pass rates (`GoldStandardAuditService`).
   - Density metrics: Claim count, evidence summary count, primary document link count, confidence scores (`Established`, `Strong`, `Moderate`, `Weak`, `Contested`).

---

### Question 2: What CANNOT currently be measured by existing instrumentation?

1. **Reader Comprehension Delta:** Whether a reader actually understood the core thesis or retained the causal explanation after reading.
2. **Claim vs. Interpretation Separation:** Whether the reader correctly distinguished between **Documented Fact** (emerald), **Historical Interpretation** (amber), and **Editorial Synthesis** (violet).
3. **Uncertainty & Historiographical Friction Recognition:** Whether the reader grasped what remains unknown or disputed among scholars.
4. **Metacognitive Assumption Shift:** Whether the reader identified a prior assumption that was complicated by evidence.
5. **Real Human Accessibility Experience:** Screen-reader fluency (NVDA/VoiceOver), keyboard navigation under real-world cognitive load, and low-bandwidth mobile degradation.

---

### Question 3: Which existing signals relate to understanding?

The following existing interaction telemetry items serve as **indirect diagnostic signals** of reader exploration:
- **Search Reformulation Count:** Reader refining query terms in `/search` to narrow down an inquiry.
- **Evidence Drawer Open Events:** Reader expanding split-view evidence panels to inspect Tier 1 primary sources.
- **Continuation Handoff Clicks:** Reader selecting "The Next Open Question" handoff links in `NarrativeReflectionBlock`.
- **Claim Attestation Tracing:** Reader descending from story text to the canonical Claim Registry.

---

### Question 4: Which signals merely measure engagement (and MUST NOT be used as comprehension proxies)?

The following conventional web metrics are **forbidden as primary success proxies**:
- ❌ **Time on Page:** May indicate confusion or distraction rather than deep learning.
- ❌ **Scroll Depth:** Measures physical gestures, not cognitive absorption.
- ❌ **Total Pageviews / Session Length:** Measures traversal quantity, not comprehension quality.
- ❌ **Click-through Rate / Return Frequency:** Measures habituation or curiosity, not understanding.

---

### Question 5: What privacy constraints apply?

In strict compliance with **Level 1 (Editorial Constitution)** and **Level 2 (AGENTS.md)** governance:
- **Zero Behavioural Profiling:** No tracking of individual reading habits across sessions.
- **Local Storage Only:** Passive memory (`tb_last_story`) is stored exclusively in `localStorage` on the reader's device.
- **No Third-Party Analytics Scripts:** No external ad-tech or cross-site tracking pixels.
- **Data Minimisation:** Telemetry logs only aggregate event types, anonymized IPs, and ISO timestamps.

---

### Question 6: What telemetry already exists and MUST be reused?

- **`PluginAnalyticsService` & `MemoryAnalyticsPlugin`** (`services/analytics/service.ts`): Primary in-memory event tracking.
- **`ObservationMonitor`** (`services/monitoring/service.ts`): System uptime, latency, and error rate telemetry.
- **`WCAGComplianceProfileBuilder`** (`lib/accessibility/compliance-projection.ts`): Automated accessibility telemetry.

---

### Question 7: What additional validation requires human testing rather than instrumentation?

Synthetic telemetry cannot measure cognitive comprehension. Evaluating whether the Narrative Operating System actually produces **understanding** requires structured human pilot testing with representative reader profiles:
- **Explorer Profile (UPSC Aspirant / Layperson):** Testing introductory orientation and narrative flow.
- **Scholar Profile (MA History Student):** Testing claim/interpretation separation and historiographical friction.
- **Researcher Profile (Journalist / Policy Analyst):** Testing direct search, primary source verification, and data interrogation.

## 4. Stop Conditions Discovered During V-1A Audit

1. **No Shadow Profiling:** Automated telemetry cannot track individual reader identities across sessions without violating Level 1 Privacy Rules.
2. **No Automated Understanding Proxy:** Synthetic automated tests can certify code execution, but CANNOT certify human comprehension. Human pilot evaluation is mandatory for Phase V-1.
3. **Governance Freeze:** No new registries, database schemas, or shadow analytics tables may be created.
