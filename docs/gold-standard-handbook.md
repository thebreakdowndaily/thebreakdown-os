# GOLD STANDARD REVIEW HANDBOOK

**Governance:** Level 1 Editorial Constitution Article XI  
**Mandatory Approval Stage:** Phase 4 before Published transition

## 7 Audit Phases & Verification Criteria

1. **Phase 1: Expert Review (Min 2 Scholars)**
   - Verification by at least two independent subject-matter experts from different interpretive perspectives.
2. **Phase 2: Reader Review (Confusion Points Audit)**
   - Tested against reader profiles (UPSC aspirant, MA History student, journalist, layperson).
3. **Phase 3: Evidence Audit**
   - Independent verification of every claim against cited primary sources by a non-author verifier.
4. **Phase 4: Bias Audit**
   - Systematic review for nationalist bias, imperial bias, presentism, and selection bias.
5. **Phase 5: Visual Audit**
   - Provenance, legality, pedagogical purpose, and WCAG AAA accessibility check for all visual assets.
6. **Phase 6: Knowledge Density Audit**
   - Target metrics: 50+ claims, 120+ evidence items, 100+ sources, 15+ documents, 10+ maps.
7. **Phase 7: Defensibility Audit ("Could We Defend This?")**
   - Every major interpretive claim mapped: Claim $\rightarrow$ Evidence $\rightarrow$ Primary Source $\rightarrow$ Disagreement $\rightarrow$ Judgment.
