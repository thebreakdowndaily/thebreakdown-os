## The Gold Standard Review (7-Phase Workflow)

No story, chapter, or major investigation may transition from **Draft / Internal Review** to **Published** without completing all seven audit phases.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE 7-PHASE GOLD STANDARD REVIEW                     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  [PHASE 1]  Expert Review         ───► Minimum 2 external scholars / specialists
       │
  [PHASE 2]  Reader Comprehension  ───► 4 reader personas (UPSC, MA, Journo, Lay)
       │
  [PHASE 3]  Evidence & Claim Audit───► 100% claim-to-primary source linkage
       │
  [PHASE 4]  Systemic Bias Audit   ───► Nationalist, imperial, hindsight checks
       │
  [PHASE 5]  Visual & Pedagogy     ───► Visual Spine verification (No empty fluff)
       │
  [PHASE 6]  Knowledge Density     ───► Density targets (Claims, sources, entities)
       │
  [PHASE 7]  Defensibility Audit   ───► "Could we defend this before a tribunal?"
```

---

### Phase 1 — Expert Review
- [ ] Reviewed by at least two subject-matter experts representing differing interpretive schools.
- [ ] Factual chronology verified against primary archives.
- [ ] Legal, constitutional, or technical terms accurately defined.
- [ ] No major scholarly counterarguments omitted.

### Phase 2 — Reader Comprehension Review
- [ ] **UPSC Aspirant Profile**: Does the piece provide clear institutional mechanisms, constitutional articles, and policy outcomes?
- [ ] **MA History / Scholar Profile**: Does the piece cite primary literature, archival accession numbers, and historiographical debates?
- [ ] **Journalist Profile**: Are dates, names, attributions, and direct quotes verifiable?
- [ ] **Informed Citizen Profile**: Can an educated layperson grasp the central issue without specialized jargon?
- [ ] "Where did you become confused?" feedback gathered and addressed.

### Phase 3 — Evidence & Claim Audit
- [ ] Every substantive assertion of fact is backed by a registered `Claim` and `Evidence` record.
- [ ] Sourcing Hierarchy respected:
  1. *Tier 1*: Primary statutory text, judicial orders, gazette notifications, official committee reports.
  2. *Tier 2*: Peer-reviewed academic literature, archival papers.
  3. *Tier 3*: Reputable specialized journalism, audited secondary sources.
- [ ] No claim relies solely on anonymous or uncorroborated single-source assertions.
- [ ] Statistical figures checked against original datasets (MoSPI, RBI, CMIE, NFHS).

### Phase 4 — Systemic Bias Audit
- [ ] **Nationalist / State Bias**: Does the piece uncritically adopt government framing without scrutinizing implementation reality?
- [ ] **Imperial / Colonial Bias**: Does historical copy reproduce colonial administrative stereotypes or unexamined tropes?
- [ ] **Presentism**: Are historical actors judged unfairly by anachronistic modern norms without historical context?
- [ ] **Hindsight Bias**: Does the text treat complex past contingencies as inevitable outcomes?
- [ ] **Selection Bias**: Were dissenting views or counter-evidence actively sought and presented?

### Phase 5 — Visual & Pedagogical Audit
- [ ] Every chart, map, or diagram teaches an essential concept (Visual Spine). Zero purely decorative stock images.
- [ ] Data visualizations include clear baseline axes, source attributions, and accessible alternative text.
- [ ] Maps accurately depict Indian statutory boundaries and include Cartographic disclaimers where mandated.

### Phase 6 — Knowledge Density Targets
- [ ] Investigation / Chapter density check:
  - Minimum 15+ canonical claims.
  - Minimum 25+ verified evidence items.
  - Minimum 20+ primary & academic sources.
  - Key thinkers / historical actors tagged with biographical summaries.
  - Next Best Understanding paths linked.

### Phase 7 — Institutional Defensibility Audit
- [ ] Ask: **"If this piece were challenged in a public scholarly forum or legal proceeding, can every claim be defended from the cited evidence?"**
- [ ] Lead Editor sign-off logged with timestamp and author signature.

## Prohibited Language Rules

The following rhetorical words are strictly banned from analytical and factual sections:
- ❌ *"clearly"*
- ❌ *"obviously"*
- ❌ *"undoubtedly"*
- ❌ *"unquestionably"*
- ❌ *"plainly"*
- ❌ *"naturally"*

**Rule:** If a conclusion is evident, demonstrate it through primary evidence; do not instruct the reader to believe it by assertion.

## Errata & Correction Protocol

1. **Immediate Logging**: When an error is identified, create an entry in the Corrections Registry within 60 minutes.
2. **Transparent Correction Notice**:
   - Place a prominent correction box at the top or bottom of the affected article.
   - Detail what was originally stated, what the verified reality is, and when the change was made.
3. **Public Corrections Ledger**: All modifications automatically appear on `https://thebreakdown.in/transparency/corrections`.
4. **Never Stealth-Edit**: Substantive factual changes must never be made silently without an errata record.
