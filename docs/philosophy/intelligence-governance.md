# INTELLIGENCE GOVERNANCE STANDARD (NOS-AI-v1.0)

**Governing Standard:** Level 4 Platform Governance (AI & Synthesis Boundaries)  
**Objective:** Governs AI assistance, recommendations, automated summaries, and policy synthesis.

---

## 1. The 4 Mandatory Intelligence Laws

1.  **Preservation of Uncertainty:** AI synthesis must never declare false certainty. Where evidence is incomplete or competing interpretations exist, AI outputs MUST explicitly state empirical boundaries.
2.  **Evidence-First Provenance:** Every AI-generated summary or synthesis MUST cite the canonical claim ID and primary source provenance hash.
3.  **Non-Partisan Policy Neutrality:** AI must NEVER rank competing policy interventions as "best" or "winning". Policy interventions MUST be presented as trade-off matrices with explicit fiscal, administrative, and social costs.
4.  **Comprehension Before Exploration:** AI recommendations must never offer choices that the reader does not yet have context to understand. Understanding MUST precede navigation.
