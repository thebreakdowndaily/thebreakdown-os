## Chapter 1 — Platform Narrative Philosophy

### 1.1 Fundamental Identity
The Breakdown is not a news website, an encyclopedia, a research database, or a commercial media platform. It is a **Knowledge Operating System** — a Narrative Intelligence Platform designed to transform fragmented information into systemic, durable understanding.

### 1.2 Category Distinctions

*   **vs. Wikipedia:** Wikipedia organizes crowdsourced static facts into neutral articles. The Breakdown projects verified canonical claims into guided, evidence-first learning journeys that explicitly map causality, uncertainty, and competing interpretations.
*   **vs. Reuters / Bloomberg:** News wire services optimize for speed, immediacy, and transactional market updates. The Breakdown rejects recency bias and optimizes for long-term structural context and epistemic health.
*   **vs. The Economist / Traditional Opinion:** Traditional commentary prioritizes persuasive argument and editorial voice. The Breakdown prioritizes the Evidence Spine — requiring every claim to be grounded in the Claim Registry and primary source provenance before narrative framing is applied.
*   **vs. Our World in Data:** Our World in Data provides static quantitative visualisations. The Breakdown integrates quantitative data directly into causal narrative trajectories connected to historical, legal, and institutional knowledge objects.
*   **vs. AI Chatbots:** Unbounded AI chatbots synthesize probabilistic text that risks hallucination and hidden uncertainty. The Breakdown bounds all intelligence strictly within the Claim Registry and Evidence Hierarchy, rendering every inference fully auditable.

> ⚠ **Tension:** Epistemic Rigour vs. Immediate Accessibility.
> Popular platforms trade epistemic rigour for rapid consumption. The Breakdown resolves this by using progressive revelation: narrative simplicity forms the surface layer, while structural evidence is instantly accessible within one to two deliberate interactions.

## Chapter 2 — Global Reader Journey

The platform governs the progression of human understanding across eight explicit stages of reader maturity:

```text
[ Anonymous Visitor ] ──► [ Curious Reader ] ──► [ Returning Learner ] ──► [ Scholar ]
                                                                                │
[ Institutional Partner ] ◄── [ Expert ] ◄── [ Contributor ] ◄── [ Researcher ] ◄┘
```

1.  **Anonymous Visitor:** First encounter. Experience focuses on resolving one fundamental uncertainty (*Why should I care?*) and establishing institutional trust.
2.  **Curious Reader:** Begins exploring related questions. The platform surfaces structural connections between topics without imposing tracking or paywalls.
3.  **Returning Learner:** The system recognizes the reader's frontier of understanding. Experience re-orients around resuming active investigations.
4.  **Scholar:** Engages with competing interpretations, historiography, and primary source documents. Utilizes split-view evidence drawers.
5.  **Researcher:** Operates in the Research Workspace. Conducts parallel claim comparisons, custom annotations, and exports versioned citations.
6.  **Contributor:** Submits formal evidence challenges or missing document attestation proposals via structured peer-review pipelines.
7.  **Expert:** Participates in the Gold Standard Review process (Phase 1 Expert Review and Phase 4 Bias Audit).
8.  **Institutional Partner:** Consumes canonical knowledge via versioned narrative APIs, academic dataset exports, and classroom learning path integrations.

## Chapter 3 — Homepage Experience ("The Beginning")

### 3.1 Constitutional Role
The homepage is not a warehouse for latest articles; it is the **Trailer to Act One** — a 4-scene narrative entry point designed to transform passive curiosity into active inquiry.

### 3.2 Prohibited Elements
The homepage must **NEVER** contain:
- Reverse-chronological news feeds ("Latest News").
- Trending/Popularity widgets ("Most Read").
- Breaking news ticker banners.
- Advertising, sponsored content, or promotional popups.
- Internal development notes, stage tags (e.g. "Scene 1", "Phase N-2"), or temporary administrative labels.

## Chapter 4 — Story World Experience

### 4.1 Ecosystem Architecture
Story Worlds are not static navigation categories; they are narrative ecosystems representing the macro-forces of human organization (*The State, The Citizen, The Planet, The Economy, The Constitution, The Future*).

### 4.2 Reader Progression & Visual Rhythm
- Each World possesses a distinct, non-distracting visual palette anchored in the Design System.
- Readers move through Story Worlds by exploring thematic gravity rather than scrolling taxonomic sub-categories.
- Story Worlds emerge dynamically from the density of nodes in the Knowledge Graph without duplicating the graph data layer.

## Chapter 5 — Story Experience

Every Story follows the mandatory **Evidence Spine Blueprint**:

```text
[ Opening Question ] ──► [ Narrative Hook ] ──► [ Evidence Progression ]
                                                        │
[ Open Question / Continuation ] ◄── [ Reflection ] ◄── [ Competing Interpretations ]
```

1.  **Opening Question:** States the precise uncertainty the story resolves.
2.  **Narrative Hook:** Establishes why this question matters to systemic understanding.
3.  **Evidence Progression:** Builds claims hierarchically, linking every assertion to Tier 1/2 sources.
4.  **Competing Interpretations:** Explicitly exposes historiographical or analytical disagreements.
5.  **Counterarguments:** Presents the strongest counter-evidence against the primary thesis.
6.  **Reflection:** Synthesizes evidence into systemic insight.
7.  **Open Question & Continuation:** Connects the story to the next logical investigation node.

## Chapter 6 — Investigation Experience

### 6.1 Lifecycle & Evolution
Investigations are macro-narratives composed of multiple interconnected stories. Investigations evolve as new canonical evidence is ingested.

### 6.2 Evidence-Driven Updating
When new primary sources contradict an existing investigation claim, the investigation surface updates transparently. The historical claim is preserved in the change ledger, and a visible **Evidentiary Update Notice** explains the revision.

---

## Chapter 7 — Knowledge Graph Experience

### 7.1 Relationship Explanation
The Knowledge Graph experience must explain relationships, not display technical node-link clutter. 

### 7.2 Contextual Zooming
Readers discover entities (People, Events, Documents, Claims, Causes, Consequences) in context. Zooming out exposes systemic connections; zooming in reveals primary document provenance.

---

## Chapter 8 — Search Experience

Search is an **Inquiry Engine**, not a document retrieval tool.

- **Query Processing:** Converts natural language queries into research questions mapped against the Claim Registry.
- **Results Hierarchy:** Renders direct evidence-backed answers, confidence ratings, and primary source links ahead of page links.
- **Ambiguity & Unknowns:** If evidence is inconclusive or missing, the Search surface explicitly states the epistemic gap rather than offering irrelevant pages.

---

## Chapter 9 — AI Experience

### 9.1 Constitutional Boundaries
AI operates strictly as a cognitive assistant bounded by the Claim Registry.

*   **AI May:** Explain dense text, simplify complex policy, compare competing interpretations, translate languages, and identify graph connections.
*   **AI Must Never:** Hallucinate claims, invent sources, speculate without evidence, hide uncertainty, or replace human editorial sign-off.

> ⚠ **Tension:** AI Conversational Ease vs. Epistemic Rigour.
> Conversational AI tends to sound authoritative even when wrong. The resolution: every AI response on The Breakdown must carry an explicit, clickable **Source Trace** listing the exact canonical claim IDs used.

---

## Chapter 10 — Reader Memory & Continuity

### 10.1 Memory Scope
The platform remembers:
- Active investigation frontier (last unanswered question).
- Examined primary sources.
- Saved bookmarks and custom collections.
- Selected Reader Mode (Explorer, Scholar, Researcher).

### 10.2 Anti-Surveillance Boundary
The platform **never** records emotional triggers, engagement loops, dwell time for ad targeting, or behavioral nudge profiles. All reader state is local-first and user-dismissible.

## Chapter 11 — Narrative Navigation

Navigation is defined by **Investigation Trajectory**, not menu trees:
- **Enter:** Orient the reader to the core question.
- **Branch:** Allow optional exploration of adjacent historical/economic concepts.
- **Resume:** Bring returning readers directly back to their learning frontier.
- **Jump/Zoom:** Enable instant transition from high-level narrative down to raw document archives.

---

## Chapter 12 — Learning Experience

The platform implements cognitive learning science principles:
- **Progressive Complexity:** Scaffolds information from foundational concepts to advanced historiography.
- **Retrieval Practice:** Offers optional, un-gamified reflection prompts at major investigation milestones.
- **Epistemic Humility:** Teaches readers to recognize when evidence is incomplete or contested.

---

## Chapter 13 — Annotation Experience

- **Claim-Linked Annotations:** Reader notes attach directly to canonical claim IDs, not floating text ranges.
- **Historical Stability:** If a claim is revised, the reader's original annotation is preserved alongside a notification of the evidentiary update.
- **Privacy Default:** All reader annotations are strictly private by default.

---

## Chapter 14 — Evidence Experience

- **Descent Rule:** Maximum 2 interactions from any prose claim down to its underlying primary source document.
- **Source Classification:** Every source displays its Tier (Tier 1 Primary Archival to Tier 3 News Report), provenance, and known analytical limitations.

---

## Chapter 15 — Comparison Experience

- **Side-by-Side Analysis:** Allows comparison of policies, leaders, historical eras, or economic models across shared evidence axes.
- **No Entertainment Rankings:** Comparative views must never generate superficial "top 10" or competitive tier lists.

---

## Chapter 16 — Data Experience

- **Argument-First Visuals:** Every chart or graph must explicitly state the claim it tests.
- **Uncertainty Legibility:** Margins of error, data gaps, and non-zero baseline justifications must be visually explicit on the chart surface.

---

## Chapter 17 — Recommendation Philosophy

Recommendations are generated **exclusively** from:
1. Unanswered questions in the current investigation.
2. Shared canonical claims and evidence.
3. Explicit reader inquiry intent.

Recommendations are **NEVER** driven by clicks, trending metrics, or sponsor placement.

---

## Chapter 18 — Community & Expert Review

- **Expert Peer Review:** External subject-matter experts contribute via structured evidence challenge protocols, not open public comment sections.
- **Transparency:** All expert review contributions and declared conflicts of interest are publicly attributed on the audited knowledge object.

---

## Chapter 19 — Accessibility Constitution (WCAG 2.2 AAA)

- **Graceful Degradation:** Full operational capability without JavaScript, animations, or hover states.
- **Motion Restrictions:** `prefers-reduced-motion` completely disables all visual transitions.
- **Screen Reader Parity:** All narrative structures, timeline links, and graph relationships are legibly articulated via semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks.

---

## Chapter 20 — Mobile Narrative Architecture

- **Single-Thread Focus:** Mobile viewports prioritize vertical narrative flow without clutter.
- **Evidence Parity:** Full access to the Evidence Drawer and primary sources via simple bottom-sheet triggers without sacrificing text readability.

---

## Chapter 21 — Motion Philosophy

- **Pedagogical Motion Only:** Visual transitions exist solely to explain spatial or causal relationships (e.g. showing how a policy connects to an outcome).
- **Prohibited Motion:** Purely decorative animations, automatic carousels, and scroll-jacking are permanently banned.

---

## Chapter 22 — Narrative Grammar

The universal building blocks across all surfaces:
- `Question` → `Hook` → `Discovery` → `Evidence` → `Counterargument` → `Reflection` → `Synthesis` → `Open Question`

---

## Chapter 23 — Narrative Rhythm & Pacing

- **Spatial Breathing:** Explicit spacing and contemplative transitions (`SceneTransition`) between dense evidentiary blocks to prevent cognitive fatigue.
- **Density Controls:** Adapts line spacing, font size, and evidence visibility based on the active Reader Mode.

---

## Chapter 24 — Anti-Patterns Blacklist

The Breakdown permanently bans:
1.  ❌ Infinite news feeds / doomscrolling.
2.  ❌ Clickbait or outrage-driven headlines.
3.  ❌ Gamified streaks or urgency popups.
4.  ❌ Hidden evidence or un-sourced assertions.
5.  ❌ Sponsored content or algorithmic engagement manipulation.

---

## Chapter 25 — Experience Certification Framework

Every new surface must pass the **5-Point Certification Audit** before deployment:

| Criterion | Test Question | Pass Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Evidence First** | Can the reader access the primary source within 2 clicks? | YES |
| **2. One Question Rule** | Does the viewport focus on resolving exactly 1 primary uncertainty? | YES |
| **3. Reader Agency** | Can the reader bypass guidance and access raw data/search freely? | YES |
| **4. Cognitive Load** | Is the surface free of decorative clutter, tickers, and popups? | YES |
| **5. Anti-Pattern Clean** | Is the surface 100% free of engagement bait and hidden claims? | YES |
