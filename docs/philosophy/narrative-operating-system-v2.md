# NARRATIVE OPERATING SYSTEM — VOLUME II (NOS-v2.0)

**Governing Standard:** Level 3 Experience System  
**Positioning:** The World's First Narrative Intelligence Platform  
**Scope:** Platform-Wide Narrative Architecture  
**Governance Status:** GOVERNANCE FROZEN. This document extends NOS-v1.0 to govern the entire platform.

---

## 4. Global Platform Experience

The Breakdown is not a website of isolated pages; it is a continuous learning journey. The reader's experience across the platform must feel like a singular, coherent investigation.

*   **The Homepage ("The Beginning"):** Sets the intent. It is the trailer that asks the defining question and routes the reader into an investigation.
*   **Story Worlds:** The macro-context. Readers do not browse "categories"; they enter interconnected ecosystems where political, economic, and historical realities collide.
*   **Investigations & Stories:** The active inquiry. Stories answer specific questions while pointing to broader systemic patterns (Investigations).
*   **The Knowledge Graph & Data Explorer:** The structural evidence. When narrative requires deep structural validation, the reader shifts to the graph without leaving the context of the story.

> ⚠ **Tension:** Narrative cohesion vs. Reader Agency.
> The platform must guide the reader naturally from a Story to an Organization, to a Timeline, to a Concept. However, narrative is guidance, not control. Readers must always be able to bypass the narrative spine to search directly, jump to the raw evidence, or browse non-linearly.

---

## 5. Narrative Navigation

Navigation must shift from page-centric retrieval to investigation-centric discovery. Navigation reveals understanding rather than maximizing clicks.

*   **Entering Journeys:** Every entry point (Search, Homepage, external link) must immediately orient the reader: *What question are we answering?*
*   **Exploration over Menus:** The primary navigation is the content itself. Links within stories are not just citations; they are doors to adjacent knowledge.
*   **Returning Readers:** The system remembers the frontier of the reader's understanding. Returning readers are not greeted with "latest news" but with "resume your investigation."
*   **Cross-Investigation Movement:** Moving from the "Economy" to the "Constitution" is not changing categories; it is zooming out to the Knowledge Graph and zooming back into a different node. 

---

## 6. Story Experience

A Story on The Breakdown is never a traditional news article. It is a structured narrative built upon the Claim Registry.

Every Story must possess this constitutional structure:
1.  **The Opening Question:** The fundamental uncertainty the story resolves.
2.  **The Narrative Hook:** Why this question matters *now*.
3.  **The Evidence Progression:** Claims presented hierarchically, grounded in primary sources.
4.  **Competing Interpretations:** Where historians or economists disagree, the tension is exposed, not hidden.
5.  **Counterarguments:** The strongest evidence against the central thesis.
6.  **Reflection:** Synthesizing the evidence into structural understanding.
7.  **The Open Question & Continuation:** Every story resolves its central premise but reveals a larger systemic question, naturally handing the reader off to the next knowledge object.

---

## 7. Investigation Experience

Investigations are the synthesis of multiple stories into a macro-narrative.

*   **Evolution:** Investigations are living entities. As new evidence is ingested into the registry, the investigation updates. It is never "finished."
*   **Evidence-Driven:** An investigation's trajectory is dictated by the Knowledge Graph, not editorial whim. If the evidence points to a contradiction, the investigation highlights the contradiction.
*   **Unresolved Questions:** Where evidence is lacking, the platform visually demarcates the boundaries of current knowledge. We map the unknown as rigorously as the known.

---

## 8. Story Worlds

Story Worlds are narrative ecosystems, not taxonomic categories. They emerge organically from the density of the Knowledge Graph.

*   **Purpose:** To show how isolated events are bound by structural forces (e.g., "The State," "The Citizen," "The Planet").
*   **Progression:** Readers do not "scroll" a Story World; they traverse its dominant themes and historical currents.
*   **Visual Identity:** Each World possesses a distinct aesthetic vocabulary (as defined in the Design System) that reinforces its thematic gravity without overriding the platform's neutrality.

---

## 9. Knowledge Graph Experience

The Knowledge Graph is the engine of understanding. Its experience must be revelatory, not intimidating.

*   **Explanation over Visualization:** The graph explains relationships. It should not look like a complex spiderweb of nodes unless the reader explicitly requests structural analysis.
*   **Contextual Discovery:** Readers discover people, events, documents, and claims organically. The fact that they are traversing a directed acyclic graph is invisible to them; they only feel the seamless connection of ideas.

---

## 10. Search Experience

Search is an **inquiry engine**, not a page-retrieval tool.

*   **Answering Questions:** When a reader searches, the engine queries the Claim Registry and Knowledge Graph to assemble an answer, backed by evidence, rather than returning a list of URLs.
*   **Prioritizing Understanding:** Results are ranked by evidentiary weight and narrative relevance, never by engagement, clicks, or recency (unless recency is the core of the inquiry).

---

## 11. AI Experience

AI is a cognitive assistant, strictly bounded by the institution's verified evidence.

**AI May:**
*   Explain complex concepts.
*   Simplify dense policy documents.
*   Compare competing interpretations.
*   Translate across languages or reading levels.
*   Connect seemingly disparate nodes in the graph.

**AI May Never:**
*   Invent evidence or hallucinate sources.
*   Generate claims not present in the Claim Registry.
*   Replace editorial judgement.

> ⚠ **Tension:** AI synthesis vs. Editorial Authority.
> Every AI response must include a transparent cryptographic or UI-level trace back to the canonical verified claims it used to generate the answer.

---

## 12. Reader Modes

Understanding is not one-size-fits-all. The platform adapts to the reader's intent without altering the underlying canonical truth.

*   **Explorer:** Optimized for narrative flow, lower density, high visual engagement. Focuses on the "What" and "Why."
*   **Scholar:** Optimized for structural connections, historiography, and competing interpretations.
*   **Researcher:** Unlocks maximum density. Bypasses narrative framing to expose the raw Claim Registry, confidence scores, and primary document metadata.

---

## 13. Narrative Grammar

The platform relies on universal narrative building blocks, applied consistently across all screens:

*   **Question:** The anchor of curiosity.
*   **Discovery:** The reveal of new context.
*   **Evidence:** The canonical proof.
*   **Counterargument:** The intellectual friction.
*   **Reflection:** The synthesis.
*   **Transition:** The connective tissue.
*   **Resolution:** The temporary reduction of uncertainty.
*   **Open Question:** The catalyst for the next journey.

---

## 14. Experience Rhythm

Pacing dictates comprehension. The platform dynamically adjusts rhythm based on context:

*   **Short Sessions:** High contrast, immediate uncertainty reduction, clear entry and exit points.
*   **Long Investigations:** Contemplative pauses (like `SceneTransition`), deep reading typography, ambient focus modes.
*   **Returning Readers:** Accelerated entry. Bypassing introductory hooks to return straight to the frontier of inquiry.

---

## 15. Recommendation Philosophy

The Breakdown does not optimize for time-on-site. We optimize for depth of understanding.

Recommendations must emerge strictly from:
*   Unanswered questions generated by the current story.
*   Shared canonical claims.
*   Shared evidentiary documents.
*   Shared entities in the Knowledge Graph.

Recommendations **never** emerge from "Most Read," "Trending," or algorithmic engagement metrics.

---

## 16. Platform-Wide Accessibility

Narrative intelligence belongs to everyone. The experience must degrade gracefully.

*   Narrative must never depend on scroll-jacking, forced timing, or complex mouse interactions.
*   Every narrative sequence must make sense when stripped of CSS and read linearly via a screen reader.
*   Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` unconditionally.

---

## 17. Narrative Anti-Patterns

The following patterns are constitutionally prohibited across all surfaces of The Breakdown:

1.  **Clickbait & Mystery-Box Storytelling:** Withholding the premise to force a click.
2.  **Infinite Feeds & Doomscrolling:** Removing natural stopping cues to trap attention.
3.  **Fake Urgency:** "Breaking news" banners for non-critical systemic issues.
4.  **Engagement Optimization:** Designing for clicks rather than comprehension.
5.  **Hidden Evidence:** Making it difficult to verify a claim's primary source.
6.  **Forced Progression:** Preventing the user from skipping ahead or reading non-linearly.
7.  **Emotional Manipulation:** Using UI or narrative framing to induce outrage or anxiety rather than clarity.

---

## 18. Narrative Certification

Every new feature, component, or narrative flow must pass the following constitutional tests before reaching production:

*   **Evidence First:** Is the raw evidence accessible within one interaction?
*   **One Question Rule:** Does this screen focus on resolving a single core uncertainty?
*   **Reader Agency:** Can the reader bypass the narrative and access the data directly?
*   **Narrative Continuity:** Does this screen naturally flow from the previous and point to the next?
*   **Understanding Gain:** Does this interaction increase systemic comprehension, or just provide a fleeting fact?
*   **No Dead Ends:** Is there a clear path forward when the immediate inquiry is resolved?

---

## 19. Long-Term Evolution

The Narrative Operating System is designed to scale from 100 claims to 10,000,000 claims without architectural redesign. 

By strictly separating the **Canonical Knowledge Layer** (Registry, Graph, Evidence) from the **Projection Layer** (Narrative, Modes, Views), the platform can evolve to support new mediums—spatial computing, voice interfaces, or AI-assisted learning—because the narrative philosophy governs *how we explain truth*, not *how we store data*.
