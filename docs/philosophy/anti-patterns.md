# INSTITUTIONAL ANTI-PATTERNS (WHAT THE BREAKDOWN IS NOT)

**Governing Standard:** Level 3 Philosophy & Constraints  
**Status:** Permanent Constitutional Ban  
**Rule:** Any feature proposal violating these constraints requires an approved Architecture Decision Record (ADR) and Level 1 Constitutional Review.

---

## The 8 Institutional Anti-Patterns

The Breakdown OS will **NEVER** become:

1.  ❌ **An Infinite News Feed:** Chronological feeds optimize for engagement, not understanding.
2.  ❌ **A Clickbait Platform:** Headlines must state research questions, not emotional triggers.
3.  ❌ **An Outrage Engine:** Polarizing conflict without evidence is strictly prohibited.
4.  ❌ **An Algorithmic Addiction Machine:** We build understanding progress, not dopamine loops.
5.  ❌ **An Opinion-First Publication:** Evidence always overrides commentary.
6.  ❌ **A Content Warehouse:** Information must exist in canonical, interconnected form.
7.  ❌ **An Engagement-Optimized Timeline:** Time-on-site is rejected as a success metric.
8.  ❌ **A Collection of Disconnected Articles:** Every story is an episode within a Season of Intelligence.
