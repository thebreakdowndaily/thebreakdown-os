# THE BREAKDOWN MISSION METRICS (NOS-METRICS-v1.0)

**Governing Standard:** Level 3 Platform Governance  
**North Star Objective:** Measure Systemic Understanding, Evidentiary Trust, and Curiosity—Never Views, Clicks, or Outrage.  
**Empirical Target Status:** Initial target metrics — subject to empirical calibration after usability testing.

---

## The 5 Mission Metrics

| Mission Objective | Operational Measurement Standard | Success Criterion Target (Initial Target) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Uncertainty Reduction** | Readers can accurately explain systemic root causes post-journey | $> 85\%$ concept clarity score |
| **2. Curiosity Continuum** | Readers voluntarily progress from completed episode to next journey | $> 75\%$ voluntary continuation rate |
| **3. Evidentiary Inspection** | Readers open `EvidenceDrawer` or inspect primary sources before concluding | $> 40\%$ evidence drawer inspection rate |
| **4. Durable Journey Retention** | Readers return to resume unfinished narrative maps ("Last Time...") | $> 60\%$ return-to-resume rate |
| **5. Reflection Completion** | Readers complete the Epilogue Reflection synthesis instead of exiting | $> 80\%$ reflection synthesis completion |

---

## Rejection of Traditional Media KPIs
The platform explicitly rejects:
- ❌ Page Views & Click-Through Rates (CTR)
- ❌ Time-on-Site / Scroll Velocity Addiction
- ❌ Viral Outrage Shares
- ❌ Algorithmic Ad Impression Triggers
