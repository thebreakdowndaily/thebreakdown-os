# THE READER CONTRACT & CONSTITUTION

**Governing Standard:** Level 3 Reader Experience System (RXS)  
**Target:** Institutional Covenant between The Breakdown and the Protagonist (Reader)

---

## The Reader Contract

```text
When you enter The Breakdown, we promise:

• We will never optimize for outrage.
• We will never reward confusion.
• We will distinguish evidence from opinion.
• We will acknowledge uncertainty.
• We will always show our reasoning.
• We will leave you understanding more than when you arrived.
```

---

## Core Reader Pillars
1. **The Reader is the Hero:** The platform does not say "Read this"—it says "Come with us."
2. **Narrative Memory:** The platform remembers what the reader has understood over time, building a persistent personal Knowledge Map.
3. **Pacing & Spatial Breathing:** Moments of silence are explicitly designed between major evidentiary revelations to allow intellectual synthesis.
