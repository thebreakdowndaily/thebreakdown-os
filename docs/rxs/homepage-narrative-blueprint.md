# THE BREAKDOWN OS — NARRATIVE OPERATING SYSTEM (RXS-v3.0)
**Governing Standard:** Level 3 Reader Experience System (RXS) & Visual Experience System (VXS)  
**Positioning:** The World's First Narrative Intelligence Platform  
**Supreme Design Constitution:** *"Every screen must reduce uncertainty while increasing curiosity."*  
**Architectural Constraint:** Frozen Canonical Knowledge Layer (`types/canonical.ts`). Zero code modifications. Pure Reader Experience Projection.

---

## 1. Core Identity & Positioning

The Breakdown is a new medium. It is **The World's First Narrative Intelligence Platform**.

*   **Traditional Journalism** delivers *isolated information*.
*   **Encyclopedias** organize *static facts*.
*   **Search Engines** retrieve *fragmented answers*.
*   **The Breakdown** guides readers through structured narrative journeys that build **durable systemic understanding**.

We do not have a "homepage"—we have **The Beginning** (The Opening Scene of Act One).

---

## 2. Supreme Design Constitution

> **"Every screen must reduce uncertainty while increasing curiosity."**

If a screen reduces uncertainty without opening a meaningful next question, the journey stalls. If it raises curiosity without resolving anything, the reader experiences frustration. The Breakdown balances both forces on every single screen.

## 5. Story Worlds Aesthetic Personas

Story Worlds are distinct visual environments rather than static categories:

*   **The State:** Deep Navy (`#0B132B`), satellite elevation contours, diplomatic cable overlays, tactical grid vectors.
*   **The Citizen:** Warm Ivory Light (`#FFFBEB`), human portraiture, spatial imagery of courtrooms, schools, and civic spaces.
*   **The Planet:** Deep Earth Teal (`#064E3B`), dynamic climate simulations, weather particle flows, satellite water dynamics.
*   **The Economy:** Dark Slate Gold (`#18181B`), macro-financial charts, trade flow vectors, supply chain paths.
*   **The Constitution:** Archival Parchment Warm Slate (`#1E293B`), legal text overlays, historical drafting manuscripts.
*   **The Future:** Quantum Void Blue (`#030712`), neural graph projections, digital infrastructure vectors.
