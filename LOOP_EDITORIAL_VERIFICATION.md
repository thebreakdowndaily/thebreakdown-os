## 1. Deep Story Audits (7-Layer Reader Experience)

### Story 1: `accountability-in-india` (When Something Goes Wrong, Who Actually Answers?)
- **30-Second Understanding:** The opening lede and summary immediately identify the central problem: India possesses a dense oversight apparatus (CAG, writ courts, vigilance commissions), yet responsibility routinely dissipates between initial administrative failure and individual consequence.
- **2-Minute Understanding:** The reader discovers the empirical baseline: CAG Report No. 4 of 2026 revealed ₹1,217.05 crore in pending liabilities under MGNREGA in Madhya Pradesh, with ₹54.79 crore stalled across 4.7 lakh wage payments due to Aadhaar-payment non-mapping, yet zero structural fixes were completed.
- **10-Minute Context & Depth:** Across 9 structured narrative acts, the story distinguishes political accountability (Shastri resigning in 1956 on constitutional principle) from criminal liability (proof beyond reasonable doubt in trial courts, as seen in the 2G spectrum trials and the Rajkot fire tragedy). It includes primary documentary extracts and interactive accountability chain diagrams.
- **Claim Verification:** 3 central claims explicitly stated, cited, and scored with explanation and source anchors.
- **Source Discoverability:** Direct links to CAG audit reports, Supreme Court judgments (`(2012) 3 SCC 1`, `Nilabati Behera`), and high court order sheets.
- **Data Comprehension:** Specific financial figures (₹1,217.05 cr, ₹564.76 cr wage liabilities) contextualized against total household participation rates (only 1.95% completing 100 days).
- **Next Step:** Meaningfully links to `mgnrega-reform` and judicial pendency reforms.

### Story 2: `mgnrega-reform` (MGNREGA 2026: The 125-Day Rural Employment Guarantee Explained)
- **30-Second Understanding:** Explains the transition on 1 July 2026 from the MGNREGA Act of 2005 to the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (Act No. 18 of 2025), expanding guaranteed days from 100 to 125.
- **2-Minute Understanding:** Clear synthesis of how the statutory guarantee changed, administrative funding splits between Centre and States, and the rationale for expanding work allotments during climate distress.
- **10-Minute Depth:** Full historical arc from 2005 enactment through the pandemic peak (COVID work demand spikes) to digital muster roll requirements and Aadhaar-based wage payment hurdles.
- **Claim Verification:** Backed by statutory gazette citations and Ministry of Rural Development annual statistics.
- **Source Discoverability:** Links to `rural.gov.in`, the Gazette of India text of Act 18 of 2025, and Parliamentary Standing Committee reports.
- **Data Comprehension:** Quantitative chart illustrating the 20-year expenditure and person-days generated trajectory.
- **Next Step:** Connects directly to rural digital payments and groundwater governance.
