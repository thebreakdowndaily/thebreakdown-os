## 2. Pacing & Rhythm Rules

- **No Uniform Card Grids**: Sections alternate between wide feature presentations, 3-column briefing cards, and clean horizontal trust bars.
- **Typographic Dominance**: Headlines lead every section with generous spacing (48px to 64px padding).
- **Subtle Dark Aesthetic**: Deep canvas `#09090B` with subtle `#18181B` surface accents and `#C9A84C` gold highlights.
