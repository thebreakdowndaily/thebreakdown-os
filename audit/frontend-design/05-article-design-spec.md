## 2. Reading Typography & Measure

- **Optimal Measure**: Max-width capped at `46rem` (68–72 characters per line) for optimal reading comprehension.
- **Line Height**: Generous `1.75rem` line-height for body paragraphs on dark canvas.
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Detailed citations and secondary appendices remain collapsible to preserve narrative flow.
