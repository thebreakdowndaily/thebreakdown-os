## 1. Core Philosophy: The Research Publication Standard

The Breakdown is designed to feel like a **serious, modern research publication**—combining the empirical rigor of *Our World in Data*, the investigative depth of *The Morning Context* and *ISignal*, the constitutional weight of *The Hindu* and *Indian Express*, and the transparency of *Wikipedia*.

It deliberately rejects SaaS dashboard tropes, generic template cards, gratuitous glassmorphism, neon badge clutter, and AI-generated visual aesthetics.

---

## 2. Five Guiding Design Tenets

### I. Evidence over Noise
- The interface exists to make primary evidence, empirical data, and statutory provenance effortless to inspect.
- UI elements never compete with the narrative or quantitative truth.
- Every claim connects to a primary source or official gazette with one click.

### II. Depth without Clutter (Whitespace over Borders)
- Structural hierarchy is established through generous typography, disciplined line heights, and deliberate whitespace rather than boxing every item inside identical cards.
- Restrained, single-pixel hairline borders (`#1F1F23`) and subtle divider rules separate sections cleanly without creating visual noise.

### III. Seriousness without Dryness (Typographic Warmth)
- Elegant editorial serif headings (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean, accessible sans-serif body typography (*Inter*) and crisp monospace metadata (*JetBrains Mono*).
- Warm gold (`#C9A84C`) and muted emerald (`#10B981`) semantic accents communicate editorial craftsmanship and authority.

### IV. Data without Dashboard Fatigue (Living Knowledge)
- Trackers are designed as **living policy products**, not business intelligence dashboards.
- Visualizations prioritize context and trend explanation over KPI-card overload.

### V. Trust without Institutional Stiffness (Radical Transparency)
- Uncertainty, methodological limits, and conflicting viewpoints are celebrated as evidence strengths.
- No algorithmic "confidence scores" that treat human knowledge like machine output.
