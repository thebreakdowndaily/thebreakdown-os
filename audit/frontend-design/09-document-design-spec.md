# Primary Document Archive & Modal Design Specification

Status: Living Specification
Date: 01 Sep 2026

---

## 1. Document Archive Principles

- **Research Archive Standard**: Documents feel like authoritative historical and legal records rather than generic file downloads.
- **Key Metadata Display**:
  - Official Document Title & Gazette Registration Number
  - Publishing Ministry / Constitutional Authority
  - Enactment / Notification Date
  - Verified Executive Summary
  - Pre-extracted Key Statutory Provisions & Section Clauses (`§`)
  - Direct Link to Official Government Gazette / Repository

---

## 2. In-App Preview Modal UX

- **Focus Management**: Traps keyboard focus on modal open; `Escape` key immediately closes the overlay.
- **Deep Linking**: Supports URL hash linking for sharing exact gazette clauses across policy research desks.
- **100% Open Access**: Never paywalls primary statutory documents or evidence trails.
