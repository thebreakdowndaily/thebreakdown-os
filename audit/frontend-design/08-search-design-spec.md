## 1. Unified Search Modal & Experience

- **Global Shortcut**: Pressing `/` or `⌘K` opens the unified modal across all pages.
- **Categorized Results**: Results are automatically clustered into:
  - 📊 **Trackers** (Direct link to living scheme tracking systems)
  - 📖 **Chapters & Stories** (Investigative explainers)
  - 🏷️ **Topics** (Broad policy domains)
  - 🏛️ **Entities** (Regulatory agencies and ministries)
  - 📄 **Primary Documents** (Official gazettes & audit reports)
- **Rich Contextual Metadata**: Displays verified claim counts, primary source counts, and publication dates directly in search results.
