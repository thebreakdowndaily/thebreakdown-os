# Mobile-First Editorial Reading Design Specification

Status: Living Specification
Date: 01 Sep 2026

---

## 1. Core Mobile Directives

1. **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44x44px touch targets across all interactive buttons, links, and modal triggers.
2. **Reading Measure**: Full-bleed reading with `16px` padding on mobile viewports; line-height scaled to `1.65` for optimal readability on handheld displays.
3. **No Horizontal Scroll on Data**: Time-series charts and metric ledgers gracefully wrap into single-column cards with horizontally scrollable semantic tables where necessary.
4. **Mobile Navigation Drawer**: Clean, full-screen mobile menu with high-contrast text and direct search bar integration.
