# Topic Knowledge Destination Design Specification

Status: Living Specification
Date: 01 Sep 2026

---

## 1. Information Architecture

Topic pages are designed as **curated knowledge destinations**, avoiding passive reverse-chronological archive lists.

```
[1. Domain Header & Scope Overview]
        │
        ▼
[2. What Matters Now (Current Regulatory / Legislative State)]
        │
        ▼
[3. Embedded Flagship Policy Trackers]
        │
        ▼
[4. Core In-Depth Explainers & Investigations]
        │
        ▼
[5. Key Institutional Entities & Regulatory Bodies]
        │
        ▼
[6. Primary Document Archive & Gazette Records]
```

---

## 2. Visual Style

- Distinct topic accent badges with restrained color coding (Emerald for Economy, Blue for Technology, Purple for Governance).
- Two-tier layout: Primary featured explainer on top followed by structured 2-column dossier cards.
