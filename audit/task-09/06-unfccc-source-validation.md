## Verified facts

1. COP29 adopted the NCQG (decision 1/CMA.6, Nov 2024), replacing the earlier **US$100 billion/year** goal.
2. **Headline goal:** at least **US$300 billion per year** in climate finance for developing countries, with **developed countries taking the lead**, by **2035**.
3. **Scale-up call:** all actors (public and private, domestic and international sources) to work toward mobilising **at least US$1.3 trillion per year** by 2035.
4. The UNFCCC expressly describes this as "tripling" the previous US$100bn goal.
5. Monitoring: UNFCCC Standing Committee on Finance is to prepare collective-progress reporting; first review expected 2028.

## What this story previously said

The climate-finance story covered India's ₹11 lakh crore annual requirement and net-zero-2070, and carried a VERIFIED claim that the $100 billion goal was first met only in 2022 (OECD). It did **not** mention COP29 / the NCQG — so there was nothing stale to correct; the NCQG is a materially new negotiating fact that belongs in the story's FAQ as a fresh-knowledge entry.

## Evidence levels

- Level A: decision lineage is documented on unfccc.int/NCQG (decisions 1/CP.21, 14/CMA.1, 9/CMA.3, 5/CMA.4, 8/CMA.5, **1/CMA.6**) and the advance-unedited decision text is public.
- Direct fetch of the registered decision PDF (FCCC/CP/2024/L.14/CMA.6 finalisation) is logged as a follow-up for the formal gazette reference.

## Decision

**ENRICH (additive, evidence-backed).** Add the NCQG sums (US$300bn by 2035, developed-country-led; US$1.3tn scale-up call; replaces $100bn goal) to the story FAQ, sourced to decision 1/CMA.6. Confidence ≥0.95; no material scholarly dispute on the headline figures (WRI/OECD agree on the numbers; dispute is only about achievability, which we do not assert).
