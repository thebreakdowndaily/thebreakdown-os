# EDITORIAL QUALITY GATES

A page cannot transition to status "published" unless it satisfies the following qualitative gates.

## 1. Originality Audit
- Does the page provide a unique angle or original dataset not present in top 5 Google results?
- Prohibited: Rewriting press releases without primary document audits.

## 2. Sourcing Verification
- Are all claims backed by primary documents or academic-grade secondary sources?
- Are links to PDFs working and pointing directly to document registry records?
- Minimum tier-1/tier-2 source count satisfied.

## 3. Neutrality & Historiography
- Are multiple viewpoints mapped (e.g. government policy vs expert critique)?
- Are arguments represented without nationalism, presentism, or emotional bias?

## 4. Internal Link Architecture
- Is the page linked from the parent cornerstone explainer?
- Does it link to at least 2 other related briefings and 2 entities?
