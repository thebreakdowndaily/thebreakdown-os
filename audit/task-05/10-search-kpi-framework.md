# SEARCH KPI METRICS FRAMEWORK

We measure performance based on organic acquisition, search positions, and deep learning journeys.

## 1. Acquisition & Visibility
- **Impressions**: Total search appearances.
- **Organic Clicks**: Clicks from SERP.
- **Organic CTR**: Normalized against average positions.

## 2. Topic Authority
- **Cornerstone Share of Voice**: Percentage of high-volume keywords ranking in top 3 positions.
- **Ranked Entities**: Total eligible entity profiles receiving organic entry traffic.

## 3. Learning & Retention (Understanding Metrics)
- **Second-Content Rate**: Percent of organic search arrivals who click to another briefing or document.
- **Newsletter Conversion**: Sign-ups initiated from high-traffic search landing pages.
