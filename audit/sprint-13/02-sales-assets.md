1. **The Question**: "How do you track whether a state has cleared its 50% PMFBY premium subsidy arrears before the kharif cut-off date?"
2. **The Tracker Surface**: Navigate to `/trackers/pmfby` $\to$ display the 10-year settlement curve and state arrears ledger.
3. **The Evidence Node**: Click the claim regarding Section 13.2 penal interest $\to$ expand the EvidenceTrail card.
4. **The Primary Document**: Click "View Document Preview" $\to$ modal opens in <2 seconds displaying the exact clause in the Revised Operational Guidelines 2024.
5. **The Data Export**: Click "View Table" $\to$ click "Export CSV" $\to$ instantaneous structured download ready for econometric modeling.
