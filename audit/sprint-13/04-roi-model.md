## 1. Core ROI Formula & Assumptions

The economic value of The Breakdown Intelligence is modeled on direct analyst time savings across a 5-seat policy research desk:

$$\text{Monthly Gross Value} = (\text{Active Seats}) \times (\text{Hours Saved / Analyst / Month}) \times (\text{Analyst Fully-Loaded Hourly Cost})$$

$$\text{Net Monthly Benefit} = \text{Monthly Gross Value} - \text{Monthly Subscription Price}$$

$$\text{Estimated ROI} = \frac{\text{Net Monthly Benefit}}{\text{Monthly Subscription Price}} \times 100\%$$

## 3. Commercial Interpretation

- Even under the most conservative assumptions (12 hours saved at ₹1,000/hr), an institutional subscription creates **₹60,000 in monthly value** against a ₹4,999 monthly cost, delivering an **11x ROI**.
- This overwhelming ROI justifies the proposed ₹4,999/month pricing and supports high long-term contract renewal rates.
