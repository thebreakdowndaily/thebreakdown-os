## 2. Structured Check-In Questionnaire

1. **What changed in your desk's research workflow this month?**  
   *Identifies new policy areas or schemes the analysts are actively researching.*
2. **Which trackers or primary document clauses did you cite in published work?**  
   *Captures external authority proof and academic citations.*
3. **Did you encounter any missing datasets or unclear statutory clauses?**  
   *Feeds directly into our editorial research queue for continuous enhancement.*
4. **Would you recommend The Breakdown Intelligence to peer research desks?**  
   *Measures customer advocacy and generates organic institutional referrals.*

---

## 3. Churn Prevention & Escalation Protocol

- **Zero Activity Trigger**: If an organization logs zero active sessions for 10 consecutive business days, the Lead Analyst is contacted with a tailored policy briefing relevant to their research domain.
- **Data Inaccuracy Escalation**: Any data discrepancy flagged by an institutional user triggers an immediate P0 review by the Knowledge Operations bureau within 4 business hours.
