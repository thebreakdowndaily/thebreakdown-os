# Flagship Knowledge Hub Specification

Status: Shipped & Active
Hubs: `/topic/[slug]`, `/trackers`, `/entity/[slug]`
Date: 31 Aug 2026

---

## 1. Architectural Philosophy

A Knowledge Hub is an authoritative synthesis surface that connects all canonical projections of an issue:
1. **Overview & Scope**: Topic description and coverage confidence score.
2. **Flagship Policy Trackers**: Immediate status, recent material changes, and metric ledgers.
3. **Cornerstone Narrative**: Evergreen explainers answering core causal and historical questions.
4. **Evidence & Document Records**: Verified claims, empirical datasets, and official gazette citations.
5. **Entity Relationships**: Nodal ministries, regulatory bodies, and corporate actors.
6. **Owned Audience Retention**: Contextual newsletter conversion and topic follow mechanisms.

---

## 2. Navigational Continuity (No Dead Ends)

Every Knowledge Hub surface guarantees at least three high-intent outbound pathways:
- **Upward**: Breadcrumbs back to the master collection or topic directory.
- **Downward**: Direct links to primary source documents and quantitative time-series charts.
- **Lateral**: Cross-links to related entities, stories, and policy trackers.
