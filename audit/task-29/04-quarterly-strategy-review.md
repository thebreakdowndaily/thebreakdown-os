## 1. Strategic Pillars & Format ROI

The Breakdown platform operations are anchored on three core pillars:
1. **Comprehension over Engagement** (pedagogical reading paths, evidence graph visualizer).
2. **Pedigree of Evidence** (canonical source registries, cryptographic provenance ledger).
3. **Structured Intent Navigation** (programmatic search and indexing across `/precedents` and `/compare` matrices).

### Format Performance Matrix
- **Deep Research Appendix (High ROI)**: Drives 80% of B2B/institutional supporter intent. Verification of source metadata and linked claims directly strengthens the evidence moat.
- **Interactive Timelines (Medium ROI)**: Enhances reading session duration, but high creation overhead requires templating optimizations.
- **Static Quick Summaries (Low ROI)**: Low user-retention value; readers bounce immediately after reading the bullet points without engaging the evidence graph.
- **Decision**: **KILL** further development of standalone static summary items. Transition all quick-mode assets to dynamic summaries populated automatically from story claims databases.

## 2. Retention, Channel & Monetization Evaluation

- **Retention Channels**: Local reading history and newsletter CTA are functioning locally. Staged rollout is required to verify conversion thresholds.
- **Monetization Structure**: 
  - Standard Paywall on Deep Mode is effective at triggering paywall telemetry.
  - Ad-supported model degrades gracefully in the absence of Google AdSense client bindings.
  - B2B Institutional license invitations validated under mock payloads.
- **Moat Development**: Our proprietary collection of verified claims and primary sources is the core differentiator. We must protect this by preventing bulk scraping of our API data endpoints while keeping sitemaps open for search engines.
