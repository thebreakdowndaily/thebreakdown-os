# Problem-Led Institutional Outreach Framework

Status: Operational
Date: 01 Sep 2026

---

## 1. Outreach Philosophy & Core Directives

1. **Zero Spam & Zero Fluff**: We never send generic marketing blasts. Every outreach note must reference an exact policy amendment, statutory circular, or state dataset relevant to the recipient's published research.
2. **Lead with Evidence, Not Software**: Showcase the exact primary document clause or 10-year scheme ledger that solves their ongoing research bottleneck.
3. **Frictionless Demonstration**: Provide a direct link to an interactive public tracker where they can immediately verify statutory citations in <10 seconds.

---
