# THE BREAKDOWN: TOPIC ARCHITECTURE & HUB TEMPLATE

Version: 1.0  
Status: Drafted  
Governance Level: 4 (Project Deliverable)  

This document defines the thematic structure of The Breakdown's topics and outlines the layout for dedicated Topic Hub pages.

---

## 1. Topic Tier Classification

The 15 active topics mapped from the repository are organized into three structural tiers to ensure SEO concentration and clean navigation:

### Tier 1: Core Strategic Pillars (Primary Navigation & Hubs)
These topics carry the highest content density and represent the main search entry points:
- **Economy**: Structural reforms, monetary policy, banking, trade, and finance.
- **Geopolitics**: India's strategic autonomy, regional security, bilateral relations.
- **Policy**: National schemes, legislative bills, governance reforms, federalism.
- **Technology**: Digital public infrastructure (DPI), semiconductors, cybersecurity.

### Tier 2: Supporting Topics (Silo Hubs)
These represent secondary verticals with growing coverage:
- **Environment**: Climate finance, renewable energy transition, water resources.
- **Agriculture**: Crop insurance schemes, farm laws, rural employment transitions.
- **Infrastructure**: Transportation networks, urban planning, logistics.
- **Judiciary**: Constitutional law, landmark verdicts, legal rights.

### Tier 3: Subtopics / Taxonomy Nodes (Tag Filters)
These are mapped as taxonomy tags rather than full hubs, preventing empty layout pages:
- **Defense** (linked to Geopolitics / Geopolitical relations)
- **Trade** (linked to Economy / Foreign policy)
- **Elections** (linked to Policy / Federalism)
- **Education** (linked to Policy / Development)
- **Healthcare** (linked to Policy / Development)
- **Cybersecurity** (linked to Technology)
- **Climate Change** (linked to Environment)

---

## 2. Topic Hub Page Template

To ensure high information density and avoid placeholder dashboards, the topic template must follow this structure:

1.  **Topic Header & Scope**: H1 topic title + a 2-3 sentence overview of what the topic covers.
2.  **Cornerstone Explainers**: 2-3 prominent, highly visible evergreen explainers on core concepts.
3.  **Latest Briefings Feed**: Chronological list of the 5 most recent briefings.
4.  **Important Entities**: A list of the top 5 actors (ministries, policies, leaders) associated with this topic.
5.  **Associated Primary Documents**: A grid of linked primary source PDFs or records referenced under the topic.
6.  **Thematic Timeline**: A chronological list of major historic milestones related to the topic.
7.  **Newsletter CTA**: Topic-specific subscriber link.

---

## 3. SEO & Knowledge Role
- **Primary SEO Role**: **Topical Pillar Landing Page**. The hub is optimized to rank for broad keyword terms (e.g. "India economy explanation", "India cybersecurity policy").
- **Knowledge Role**: **Knowledge Directory**. Acts as a thematic index page, grouping dynamic briefings with evergreen historical chapters to maximize internal linking.
- **Indexability**: Set to `index, follow` with canonical URL absolute to `/topic/[slug]`.
