## 1. The Breakdown Moat Hierarchy

Traditional media relies on temporal ephemeral articles that depreciate in value within 48 hours. The Breakdown builds a compounding **Knowledge Capital Moat**:

```
Level 4: Verified Multi-System Policy Trackers (Living Ledgers)
Level 3: Primary Official Document Archive & Clause-Linked Modals
Level 2: Longitudinal Time-Series Data Models with Semantic Fallbacks

```

## 2. Competitive Replicability Matrix

1. **Claim & Evidence Provenance Layer**: Every major assertion is backed by confidence scores and direct source links. Competitors cannot easily backfill 5,000+ structured knowledge relationships without severe editorial restructuring.
2. **Living Policy Trackers (`/trackers/*`)**: Replaces transient news posts with persistent, stateful URLs that accumulate updates over decades (e.g. 20-year MGNREGA budget progression, 10-year PMFBY settlement ratios).
3. **Primary Document Experience**: In-app previews extract exact statutory sections and gazette clauses, keeping researchers and students on-platform.
4. **Clean Performance & Type Safety**: Zero-dependency pure SVG vector chart engine maintaining 227 kB shared bundle size.
