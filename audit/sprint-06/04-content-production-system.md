## 1. Production Stage Flow

```
1. Search Opportunity Identification
        ↓
2. Production Brief Creation
        ↓
3. Primary Research & Statutory Sourcing
        ↓
4. Canonical Claim Extraction (types/canonical.ts)
        ↓
5. Evidence & Provenance Audit
        ↓
6. Draft Composition (Orientation → Chapters → FAQ)
        ↓
7. Gold Standard Review (Accuracy, Bias, Defensibility)
        ↓
8. Bidirectional Linking (Hub, Story, Tracker, Entity, Documents)
        ↓
9. Multi-Channel Distribution Packaging
        ↓
10. Refresh & Freshness Queue
```

## 2. Mandatory Content Brief Template

Every research explainer requires a pre-publication brief containing:
1. **Primary User Question**: Concrete query being addressed.
2. **Direct Answer (The Short Version)**: 2-sentence summary answering the central finding.
3. **Core Statutory Provisions**: Exact legislative acts, gazette numbers, or circular IDs.
4. **Empirical Dataset**: Official time-series or institutional data table.
5. **Scholarly / Institutional Perspective Disagreements**: Acknowledged counterpoints.
6. **Cross-System Links**: Target Tracker, Target Entity Hub, and Topic Hub.
7. **Contextual Newsletter CTA**: Specific briefing value proposition.
