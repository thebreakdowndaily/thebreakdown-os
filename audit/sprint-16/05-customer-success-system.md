Status: Operational
Date: 01 Sep 2026
Governance: AGENTS.md v1.0 — Platform Beta / Commercial Doctrine

## 1. Customer Success Lifecycle Architecture

```
[1. Onboarding (<8 Min)]
  ├── Provision organizationId & 5 seat invite tokens
  └── 3-step orientation: Search → Clause Preview → CSV Export
        │
        ▼
[2. Activation (Week 1)]
  ├── 100% seat claim rate target across desk
  └── First research brief or working paper citation verified
        │
        ▼
[3. Weekly Engagement Monitoring]
  ├── Track active sessions, clause lookups, and CSV exports via telemetry
  └── Automated check if zero sessions logged for 7 consecutive days
        │
        ▼
[4. Monthly Value Briefing (Day 30)]
  ├── Deliver lightweight Monthly Institutional Report
  └── Highlight hours saved, new gazette releases, and time-series updates
        │
        ▼
[5. Issue & Request Governance]
  ├── Resolve data freshness or citation questions within 24 hours
  └── Filter feature requests through canonical registry rules
        │
        ▼
[6. Renewal Review (Day 300)]
  ├── 15-minute executive review with Desk Director
  └── Revalidate annual desk ROI and execute Year-2 renewal agreement
```

---

## 2. Key Operational Principles

1. **Zero Unnecessary Meetings**: Never force busy policy fellows into long status calls. Deliver value asynchronously through clean reports and instant in-app data.
2. **Preserve Editorial Credibility**: Customer success interactions must never compromise editorial independence or empirical neutrality.
