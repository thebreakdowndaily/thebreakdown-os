# Institutional Renewal Readiness & Churn Prevention Framework

Status: Completed
Date: 01 Sep 2026

---

## 1. 30-Day Value Review Cadence

To guarantee high institutional contract renewals, the following 30-day review cadence is established:

```
Day 1: Onboarding & 5-Seat License Setup
         ↓
Day 7: First Research Task Check-in (Verify CSV download & Clause Previews)
         ↓
Day 14: Mid-Trial Usage & Value Review (Review hours saved & active sessions)
         ↓
Day 25: Commercial Renewal Proposal & Procurement Confirmation
         ↓
Day 30: Contract Signature & Ongoing Monthly Briefing Delivery
```

---

## 2. Renewal Risk Indicators & Mitigation

| Risk Indicator | Early Warning Signal | Proactive Mitigation Playbook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Seat Utilization** | < 2 active users logging in during first 14 days | Direct analyst onboarding demo focusing on their active policy research topic |
| **Zero CSV Downloads** | Organization browsing narrative but not exporting data | Send curated CSV data package with pre-formatted Excel models for their desk |
| **Stale Data Concern** | Inquiries regarding recent gazette updates | Immediate briefing highlighting newly indexed circulars and CAG clauses |

---

## 3. Product Roadmap Governance for B2B

- **Strict Multi-Customer Rule**: No custom features are built unless requested by $\ge 2$ independent institutions.
- **Canonical Architecture Integrity**: All B2B deliverables must originate from the platform's core registry models (`claims`, `evidence`, `sources`, `documents`, `trackers`), preventing parallel codebases.
