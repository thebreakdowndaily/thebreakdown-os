# Master Commercial Terms & Conditions — The Breakdown Intelligence

Status: Standard Draft (LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED prior to enterprise contracting)
Date: 01 Sep 2026

---

## 1. Definitions & Service Scope
- **"Service"**: Refers to The Breakdown Knowledge Platform web application, including living policy trackers, primary document clause extraction, data exports, and research feeds.
- **"Customer"**: The subscribing institution, think tank, university department, or corporate research desk.
- **"Authorized Users"**: Up to five (5) named employees or researchers affiliated with the Customer.

---

## 2. Permitted Data Use & Citation Rights
- **Internal Analysis**: Customer may freely use downloaded CSV datasets, policy ledgers, and time-series for internal research, forecasting, and policy analysis.
- **External Publication & Citation**: Customer may cite figures and charts in published research papers, reports, and briefings, provided attribution is formatted as:  
  *Source: The Breakdown (https://thebreakdown.in/trackers/[slug])*
- **Resale Restriction**: Customer shall not resell, bulk scrape, or redistribute raw underlying databases as a standalone commercial data feed to third parties without prior written consent.

---

## 3. Subscription, Billing & Cancellation
- **Term**: Subscriptions operate on a monthly or annual auto-renewing basis.
- **Payment**: Payable in INR within thirty (30) days of invoice date.
- **Cancellation**: Customer may cancel at any time with thirty (30) days notice prior to the end of the billing period.

---

## 4. Confidentiality & Security
- **Organization Isolation**: The Breakdown maintains strict multi-tenant isolation. No customer query logs, research workspace data, or custom annotations shall be exposed to other organizations.
- **Zero PII Leakage**: Telemetry logging operates exclusively with anonymized identifiers.

---

## 5. Service Level & Support
- **Uptime**: Platform maintains a 99.9% uptime target for static tracker and document surfaces.
- **Inquiries**: Technical and data verification inquiries receive responses within twenty-four (24) business hours.
