## 1. Executive Summary

Policy researchers, financial analysts, and economists spend dozens of hours every month manually scouring government portals, disparate PDF circulars, and unformatted statistical bulletins to track central policy developments.

**The Breakdown Intelligence** solves this through structured, registry-backed knowledge assets:
1. **Living Policy Trackers**: Stateful policy monitoring (e.g. MGNREGA 125-day reform, UPI regulatory limits, Semiconductor Mission capex disbursements, and PMFBY claims settlement ratios).
2. **Primary Document Extraction**: In-app previews extracting exact statutory sections, gazette clauses, and CAG audit paragraphs with zero third-party editorial spin.
3. **Longitudinal Time-Series & Clean Data Exports**: Instant CSV data downloads and API feeds backed by official government databases.
4. **Statutory Change Feeds**: Immediate alerts on gazette notifications, ministerial orders, and penal interest triggers.
