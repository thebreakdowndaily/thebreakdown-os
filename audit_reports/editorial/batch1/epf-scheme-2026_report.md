- **[CLM-EXT-EPF-SCHE-049]** (`faq`) *What changed for pension (EPS)?: EPS 2026 replaces EPS-95 and the 1971 Family Pension Scheme.   - Type: FACTUAL_ASSERTION | Registered Match: None (Unregistered) | Support: MODERATE
