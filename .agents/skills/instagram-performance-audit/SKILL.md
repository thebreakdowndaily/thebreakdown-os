# Instagram Performance Audit

## Useful inputs
Publication date; format; reach; views/plays; non-follower reach; retention/watch metrics; likes; comments; saves; shares; profile visits; follows; link activity; and account audience at publication. Use only metrics actually supplied.

## Workflow
1. Record data source, period, missing fields, context, and whether data comes from native Insights or another tool.
2. Check denominators and metric definitions. Views are not unique people reached; do not combine incompatible metrics.
3. Compare posts to the account's own baseline and segment by format, topic, objective, age, and distribution opportunity where possible.
4. For small samples, state that results are descriptive and not causal. Show counts, denominators, and formulas.
5. Diagnose the likely bottleneck—initial attention, retention, saves/shares, profile conversion, or downstream traffic—and tie the diagnosis to observed data.
6. Separate observed facts, hypotheses, and recommendations. Do not claim secret knowledge of the ranking system.
7. Propose a small experiment set with one variable per test, comparison group, measurement window, and decision rule.
8. Feed results into the content calendar. A single outlier is a hypothesis, not a universal rule.

## Output
Baseline and data-quality notes; metrics/formulas; strongest and weakest posts with context; patterns and counterexamples; limitations; prioritized experiments; next review date.

## Safety
Never invent missing Insights, scrape accounts at scale, log in, or expose private individual-level audience information.
