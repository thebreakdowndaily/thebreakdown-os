# Instagram Caption

## Workflow
1. Identify the caption's primary job: explain, contextualize, invite reflection, earn a save/share, or direct readers to the full story.
2. Draft three first-line options. Use a concrete question, finding, contradiction, or consequence only when the evidence supports it.
3. Add context rather than merely describing what the image already shows.
4. Include qualifications needed to avoid misleading impressions. Attribute allegations and estimates clearly.
5. Choose one useful CTA; do not reflexively demand likes, comments, saves, and shares together.
6. Use natural topic keywords only where relevant. Never fabricate search volumes or stuff keywords.
7. Treat hashtags as optional labels. Check current official Meta guidance before asserting a hard limit or algorithm effect.
8. Verify every number, proper noun, quote, date, source reference, and URL against the approved source brief.
9. Add accessibility guidance when important meaning is conveyed visually or through audio.

## Output
Goal and reader; three opening lines and selection rationale; final caption; one CTA; optional relevant labels; claim/source checklist; accessibility note; status DRAFT or NEEDS VERIFICATION.

## Do not
Do not invent quotations, statistics, citations, trending hashtags, reach forecasts, or algorithm claims. Do not describe a non-clickable caption URL as clickable. Never schedule or publish.
