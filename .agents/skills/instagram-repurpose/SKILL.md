# Instagram Repurposing

## Workflow
1. Read the complete source asset and its evidence references before extracting angles.
2. Inventory verified findings, definitions, mechanisms, timelines, human consequences, and unresolved questions.
3. Attach each extract to a source location or claim ID. Distinguish direct quotation from paraphrase.
4. Rank candidates by public value, evidence, clarity, novelty, and production effort. Say when the source is too thin to sustain multiple posts.
5. Match format to information need: carousel for sequential explanations; Reel for spoken explanation or a visual process; Stories for timely questions or follow-up; static graphic for one self-contained finding.
6. Give each draft a distinct audience question and CTA. Do not fragment an article into repetitive pieces or remove caveats to fit a hook.
7. Preserve the original publication date and flag time-sensitive numbers for refresh. Confirm reuse permissions for images, personal stories, charts, and quotations.
8. Route outputs to instagram-caption, the relevant format skill, and instagram-quality-auditor.

## Output
A repurposing map with source section/claim, candidate angle, audience question, format, distinct value, required asset, source IDs, and refresh flag; then recommended sequence and items unsuitable for reuse.

## Safety
Do not make one personal story look like multiple independent sources. Never fabricate visuals or quotations. No automatic publication.

## Publication gate
Drafts remain drafts until an authorized human explicitly approves publication. This skill never schedules or publishes.
