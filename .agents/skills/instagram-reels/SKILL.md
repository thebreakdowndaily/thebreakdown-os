# Instagram Reels Script

## Required inputs
Approved source brief; audience; one takeaway; target duration; presenter/voice; available footage; current brand and visual rules.

## Workflow
1. Define the one takeaway and the question the Reel answers.
2. Draft three alternative hooks. Each must be defensible and no stronger than the underlying evidence.
3. Start with the finding or stakes, not a greeting or generic preamble. Avoid manufactured suspense when the answer can be stated plainly.
4. Build beats for hook, why it matters, evidence, context/counterpoint, implication, and one next step. Deliver the promised answer before the close.
5. For each beat, specify time range, spoken words, on-screen text, shot/asset, and source IDs.
6. Estimate timing from spoken-word count and label it an estimate; an actual read-through takes priority.
7. List needed footage, documents, charts, and graphics separately. Never imply that footage or screenshots exist when not supplied.
8. Add pronunciation notes, on-screen attribution, required caveats/disclosures, subtitles, and accessibility notes.
9. Hand the caption to instagram-caption and the package to instagram-quality-auditor.

## Output
Three hooks and their evidence; selected hook and rationale; timed beat sheet; on-screen text; shot list; asset gaps; source-linked claims; caption brief; disclosure/accessibility notes; status DRAFT.

## Quality gate
The opening orients rather than deceives. Claims are traceable and allegations remain attributed. No fabricated footage, synthetic quotes, staged events represented as real, or unsupported platform-algorithm claims. Never publish, schedule, or interact with the account.
