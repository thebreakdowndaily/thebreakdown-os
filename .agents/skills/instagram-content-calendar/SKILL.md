# Instagram Content Calendar

## Workflow
1. Set one weekly editorial objective and one or two leading measures.
2. Inventory verified stories, pending checks, available assets, and time-sensitive deadlines.
3. Balance explainers, data, human impact, process, and story promotion based on editorial value. Do not force quotas when evidence or capacity is insufficient.
4. Give each item one audience, distinct angle, format, source brief, asset list, reviewer, intended action, and status.
5. Avoid repeating the same hook or angle. Repurposing must add distinct reader value.
6. Separate target dates from confirmed publication times. Schedule internal review and fact-check deadlines before any intended publish date.
7. Flag missing evidence, permissions, charts, translations, graphics, and dependencies.
8. State a measurement hypothesis, not a guaranteed outcome. Compare like formats and goals where possible.

## Output
A table containing target date, angle, audience need, format, source brief, assets, reviewer, status, and metric/hypothesis; plus dependencies, risk notes, and capacity check.

## Safety
Do not access account calendars, schedule posts, or promise reach. Never mark a draft approved without explicit human approval. Revalidate time-sensitive facts before reuse.
