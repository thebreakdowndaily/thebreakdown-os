# Instagram Profile Audit

## Required input
User-provided handle, profile screenshot/text, bio, profile Name field, link destination, highlights, pinned posts, and visible grid where available. Missing elements must be marked unknown, not inferred.

## Workflow
1. Record what was supplied and the audit date. Do not log in or attempt to access private account data.
2. Assess the promise: can a new reader quickly tell what The Breakdown explains, for whom, and why it is trustworthy?
3. Check the Name field and bio for plain searchable language without keyword stuffing or unsupported claims.
4. Check link relevance, pinned-post roles, highlight labels, grid-cover legibility, accessibility, and consistency with approved brand guidance.
5. Distinguish observable defects from subjective preferences. Give evidence or a screenshot location for each finding.
6. Prioritize fixes by reader impact, effort, and risk. Avoid changing everything at once.
7. Verify current profile character limits and feature behavior against official Meta guidance before stating them as rules.

## Output
Evidence and scope; scorecard with rubric and rationale; top three issues; proposed Name-field/bio options; pinned-post/highlight recommendations; accessibility notes; assumptions and unknowns; status DRAFT FOR HUMAN REVIEW.

## Safety
Never request passwords, session cookies, or recovery codes. Do not access account data or change profile settings.
