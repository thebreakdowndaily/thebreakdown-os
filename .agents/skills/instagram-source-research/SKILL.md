# Instagram Source Research

## Workflow
1. Define the question, public-interest relevance, audience, as-of date, and intended format.
2. Inventory sources. Prefer original laws, orders, datasets, studies, filings, transcripts, official records, or direct statements for what they can establish.
3. Use credible independent reporting to corroborate context, find counterevidence, and locate original documents. An official announcement proves what an institution announced; it does not automatically prove every claim in it.
4. Break the draft into atomic factual claims. For each, record exact evidence, source URL, publisher, publication or update date, access date, and a page/table/paragraph locator where available.
5. Verify that the source actually supports the claim. A working URL alone is not verification. Check dates, geography, denominator, methods, definitions, revisions, and scope.
6. Separate verified facts, attributed allegations, official claims, interpretation, estimates, projections, and unresolved disputes.
7. Search for meaningful counterevidence. Include the strongest relevant qualification and note when reliable sources disagree.
8. Recommend up to three story angles based on public value, evidence strength, distinctiveness, and clarity—not virality alone.

## Required output
- Research question and as-of date
- Source inventory and limitations
- Claim ledger: claim, exact support, URL, date, status, caveat
- Verified facts and attributed claims
- Counterevidence and uncertainty
- Candidate angles and recommended lead
- Claims needing human verification
- Recommendation: DRAFTABLE or NEEDS VERIFICATION

## Status labels
VERIFIED; PARTIALLY VERIFIED; ATTRIBUTED ONLY; CONTESTED; UNVERIFIED.

## Safety
Never invent sources, quotations, numbers, or dates. Do not expose private personal data. Apply stronger corroboration and careful attribution to allegations about identifiable people, legal matters, elections, health, finance, or public safety. Stop at the evidence brief; do not publish or contact subjects.
