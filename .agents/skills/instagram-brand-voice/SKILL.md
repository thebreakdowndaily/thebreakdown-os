# Instagram Brand Voice

## Purpose
Keep drafts recognizably The Breakdown without imitating another creator or turning journalism into engagement bait.

## Workflow
1. Read the existing Instagram command and channel formatter before writing. Use approved project files as the operational source of truth.
2. Identify the intended reader, what they should understand, and the useful action after reading.
3. Derive voice rules from approved published examples; label unsupported observations as hypotheses.
4. Prefer plain language, precise verbs, short paragraphs, specific context, fair attribution, and explicit uncertainty.
5. Separate facts, analysis, estimates, and attributed claims. Preserve meaningful disagreement.
6. Avoid rage bait, false urgency, exaggerated certainty, generic AI phrasing, and claims of exclusive knowledge without evidence.
7. Flag conflicting brand instructions. Do not silently change colors, logo, typography, aspect ratio, or templates.

## Output
Audience; voice anchors; sentence rhythm; preferred language; anti-patterns; evidence and attribution style; CTA style; supported examples; uncertainties; approval status.

## Quality gate
Every asserted voice trait must be grounded in approved examples or labelled a hypothesis. Do not copy a third party's signature language. Draft guidance only; never publish.
